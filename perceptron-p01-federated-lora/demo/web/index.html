    Read the numbers this way

    • Full-system comparison under paired availability. Every strategy saw the same dropouts and cut-offs; AnchorLoRA's ingredients act together, so no single one is credited here.
    • Norm share is a magnitude diagnostic: how large each device's piece of the update was. It is not a fairness guarantee.
    • Outage results hold at the evaluated rounds (3, 6, 9, 12 committed; every round live). Nothing is claimed between checkpoints.
    • Device budgets are simulated: ranks, step counts and availability are assumptions; every device runs on this Mac. The model and the text are real.
    • The live panel is a reduced configuration (batch 1×64 tokens, 8 held-out blocks × 64 tokens per domain, 6 rounds). Its values are deltas against the untuned model on the same subset and are not comparable in absolute terms to the committed runs. Its pattern was pre-registered and checked on 5 seeds before the demo: –
    • The unplug is in-process: a Flower node removed from an in-process grid (LocalGrid.kill_node), not a killed OS process.
