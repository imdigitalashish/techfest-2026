**Scope:** verifies the SAM/MAM/growth-faltering escalation timelines in `components/action_router/protocol.yaml` (all marked `VERIFY`) against primary national protocol/guideline text.
**Protocol:** every quoted passage below was opened by this research pass from the cited URL on the access date shown. No text is quoted from memory. Where primary text could not be opened, the item is marked **UNVERIFIED**.
**"Today" = 4 October 2026. Access date for all sources: 2026-10-04.**

**Status legend:** **Yes** = primary text opened, quote matches/supports our assumption. **Partly** = primary text opened but only partially supports, or materially qualifies, our assumption. **No** = primary text contradicts our assumption. **UNVERIFIED** = no primary text opened this session.

## (c) UNVERIFIED items

1. **"LS escalation if SAM not appraised by day 7"** (`CMAM-2023-SAM-LS-day7`) — no day-7 Lady-Supervisor trigger found anywhere in the CMAM protocol (1,618 lines), Saksham/Poshan 2.0 guidelines (3,221 lines), HBYC handbook (3,576 lines), or the 2011 NRC Operational Guidelines (3,360 lines). The closest verified LS duty is a **general monthly oversight/audit role** (VHSNC + Lady Supervisor + CDPO, "on monthly basis" — Saksham/Poshan 2.0 p.20), not a child-specific day-7 trigger. UNVERIFIED as a distinct rule across all 4 sources opened.
2. **"CDPO + MO-PHC joint escalation at day 14"** (`CMAM-2023-SAM-day14`) — CDPO/Supervisor review of SAM/MAM status is confirmed as a role, but its cadence ("Sector, projects meetings") is never given a day count in any of the 4 sources opened. UNVERIFIED.
3. **"MAM → counselling + SNP within 7 days"** — protocol implies prompt/same-cycle enrollment, not a stated 7-day figure, in both CMAM and Poshan 2.0 texts. UNVERIFIED for the specific number.
4. **"LS review at 30 days if MAM not improving"** — official non-improvement triggers are 2 consecutive weeks (clinical, CMAM) or 3 months (programmatic "non-recovery," CMAM), not 30 days, and are not named as an "LS" step in any source opened. UNVERIFIED/contradicted.
5. **"Growth faltering → AWW home visit within 7 days" as a flat-curve (stagnation) rule** — HBYC gives "immediate talk to AWW/ANM + follow-up for compliance" with **no day-count** for flat-curve faltering (only the downward-curve/severe case has an explicit "5 days"). The flat-curve 7-day figure remains UNVERIFIED.
6. **Central Empowered Programme Committee (EPC) and State Level Steering Committee meeting frequency** — both documents (Saksham/Poshan 2.0, pp.8-9) name the chair (Secretary MoWCD; Chief Secretary) but state no numeric cadence ("regular basis" only). Recorded as "not specified," distinct from "not opened."
7. **CDPO "Sector, projects meetings" frequency** (block/sector level) — role confirmed (CMAM p.20), cadence not stated in any source opened.
8. State-specific ICDS supervisory manuals that might contain a literal day-7/day-14 SAM escalation ladder were **not searched this session** (out of time budget) — flagged as the most likely place such a ladder could be officially documented, if it exists at all outside Poshan-Setu's own design.
