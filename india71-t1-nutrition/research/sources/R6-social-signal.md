## 1. Key Findings (draft — will refine)

### What's new 2025–2026
- Mandatory **Face Recognition System (FRS)** for Take-Home Ration (THR) distribution via Poshan Tracker — order dated 30 May 2025, effective **1 July 2025**; FRS at registration from **1 Aug 2025**. *(Verifying against wcd.gov.in circular and PIB.)*
- By Jan/Feb 2026, government claims ~52–97% of beneficiaries e-KYC'd / face-captured (numbers vary by source/date — needs reconciliation).
- Nominee module added for beneficiaries unable to appear in person (one-time e-KYC + per-transaction facial verification for nominee).
- Children under 6 (no biometric Aadhaar) verified via parent/guardian face.
- All Anganwadi Centres (AWCs) reportedly on Poshan Tracker by Feb 2025; >10.12 crore beneficiaries registered.

### Top pain points (evidence strength noted)
1. **Duplicate work (digital + physical registers)** — STRONG (multiple independent news investigations: BehanBox, The Print, ground reports from Tamil Nadu).
2. **Device/network failures** — STRONG (Tribune, Times of India Ludhiana, Hindustan Times Chandigarh — multiple states: Punjab, Haryana).
3. **FRS impracticality / exclusion risk** — STRONG (Hindustan Times Chandigarh Nov 2025 report citing AWW statements; legal challenge in Bombay HC).
4. **Data-quality gap vs NFHS-5** — STRONG (Telegraph India, News18 cite named researchers; parliamentary responses acknowledge administrative-vs-survey data differences).
5. **Legal/union escalation** — MODERATE-STRONG (Bombay High Court petition reported by The Hindu — verifying case name/status).
