# L7 — What Is Deployed Today in India: State/District Nutrition Campaigns, ICDS-CAS Evaluation, and Why the Gap Persists

**Research status:** IN PROGRESS (incremental — being appended every 2–3 sources)
**Access date for all sources:** 2026-10-04
**Compiled for:** India @71/100 Challenge, Theme 1 — Maternal & Early Childhood Nutrition
**Purpose:** "Existing ecosystem analysis" — what is deployed, how data becomes action, evaluation evidence, and documented reasons the data→action gap persists.

---

## 1. Key Findings (draft — will refine as sources are added)

1. **State campaigns are periodic "sprint" drives layered on top of ICDS, not continuous data-governance systems.** UP's Sambhav Abhiyan selects the 100 highest-stunting Anganwadi Centres (AWCs) per district (7,500 statewide) for intensive nodal-officer monitoring and monthly District Nutrition Committee review — a manual, campaign-cycle escalation mechanism, not an always-on early-warning layer [2].
2. **The one rigorously published impact evaluation of a nutrition data system (ICDS-CAS/CommCare, Patil et al. 2022, BMJ Global Health)** found a CommCare-based app for frontline workers improved *process* metrics frontline workers directly control (home-visit timeliness +7.9–8.3pp, counselling recall +8.0–11.5pp, growth-monitoring coverage +22pp in Bihar only) but had **"no effects... on infant and young child feeding practices"** — i.e., digitizing the worker's task list did not translate into changed caregiver behaviour or outcomes, because those depend on systemic/supply factors the app cannot fix [1].
3. **Tribal/high-burden pockets show the data→action loop failing even where a dashboard (Poshan Tracker) and a facility-level escalation structure (Village Child Development Centres, VCDCs) already exist.** In Melghat (Maharashtra), Poshan Tracker recorded 1,82,443 malnourished children statewide (Feb 2025: 30,800 SAM + 1,51,643 MAM) and VCDCs exist in every gram panchayat to monitor SAM cases, yet the Bombay High Court found the government response "extremely casual" after 65 infant deaths (June–Nov 2025) and ~220 children in SAM status with ~50% at risk of death absent intervention — officials attribute this to departments (health, tribal, WCD, finance) "operat[ing] in silos," poor connectivity delaying emergency referral, and chronic medical-staff shortages, not to absence of data [4].
4. Community-led, low-tech models (Assam's Project Sampoorna, "Buddy Mother" peer pairing + Google Sheets/WhatsApp tracking) show large recovery-rate claims (~95.6% reduction in malnutrition in Bongaigaon within a year per secondary reporting) and won national recognition, but scale/replication evidence beyond the originating district is thin and not independently audited in what I've verified so far [3] — **UNVERIFIED at evaluation-rigor level**, flagging for cross-check against an independent (non-GoI, non-campaign) source.

## 3. Why-the-Gap-Persists Table

| Cause | Evidence (quote) | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Departmental silos at the point of action**, even with facility-level monitoring (VCDCs) and dashboard data (Poshan Tracker) already in place | Experts working in Melghat told The Hindu that "the multiple government departments that execute programmes here operate in silos, resulting in inconsistent supplement delivery, irregular monitoring, and weak coordination to sustain implemented policies." | [4] |
| **Digital tooling improves worker-controlled process metrics but cannot fix systemic/structural service gaps** | "The at-scale app integrated with ICDS improved provision of services under the purview of CHNWs but not those that depended on systemic factors, and was relatively more effective when baseline levels of services were low. Overall, digitally enabling CHNWs can complement but not substitute efforts for strengthening health systems and addressing structural barriers." | [1] |
| **Last-mile infrastructure (roads, electricity, healthcare staffing) undermines even well-monitored SAM caseloads** | The Hindu reports Melghat's "peripheral issues" include "lack of proper roads to reach a hospital in time, inadequate electricity supply to homes, lack of primary healthcare centres (PHC)," plus a high doctor "absconding rate" — tribal activist Bandu Sane: "The roads are in poor condition and not maintained. Why would anyone work if the government fails to provide basic civic facilities?" | [4] |
| **Campaign-based escalation (nodal officers, monthly committee review) is manual and periodic, not a continuous automated early-warning trigger** | UP's Sambhav Abhiyan 5.0 relies on "100 officers of block and district level" appointed as nodal officers who physically inspect centres, with stunting status "reviewed in the District Nutrition Committee meeting every month" — a monthly human review cycle, not real-time alerting | [2] |

## 4. What We Should Borrow / Where We Can Do Better

*(draft — to be finalized after CAG/PSC sources are added)*

- **Borrow:** UP's model-centre peer-emulation incentive (publicly naming improved AWCs) and district-committee review cadence as a *minimum viable* human-in-the-loop escalation pattern — but our system should convert the monthly manual review into a continuously-updated risk queue so nodal officers triage by exception rather than waiting for the campaign cycle.
- **Borrow:** Assam's "Buddy Mother" peer-accountability layer as a cheap last-mile *action* mechanism that a data layer could auto-trigger/match (e.g., auto-pairing on SAM flag) rather than relying on one district's manually organized pilot.
- **Do better than Melghat:** our design should treat "data exists but departments don't act together" as the central failure mode to solve for — i.e., the deliverable isn't another dashboard, it's a forcing function (SLA-bound task routing across health/WCD/tribal departments) that a single-department dashboard like Poshan Tracker structurally cannot provide.
- **Do better than ICDS-CAS:** since the published evidence shows digitizing frontline worker tasks alone doesn't move child-feeding outcomes, our value-add framing should explicitly target the *systemic/convergence* layer (cross-department escalation, supply-chain/referral triggers) rather than re-building another worker-facing data capture app.
