## Hard questions (from the jury review), with honest answers

- **"Are you accusing Anganwadi workers of hiding wasting?"** No. 19.3% and 5.46% come from different instruments, periods and denominators. It's an unexplained discrepancy, not an under-detection rate. We propose blinded re-measurement and AWC/device-level support diagnostics, never worker scores.
- **"Where is the department boundary, and why accept wrong links?"** It's implemented and tested: each department runs `encode_department`, and the matcher (`match_encoded`) accepts only bit-strings and keyed tokens. It reproduces the committed result exactly. The matcher still sees which records share a token, and Bloom filters can be attacked, so the key stays with the departments and hardening is a pilot item. Wrong links: at the loose threshold, 366 against 34 for pooled names. At matched precision it's 31 against 34, but that's an in-sample comparison on this synthetic cell; the operating threshold is a proposed policy, to be set on pilot data with clerical review. The cohort on screens 4–7 is linked at the default 0.91.
- **"Your child: what did you predict, and does fitting prevalence validate it?"** Her first-ever episode, in simulated data. The rule lists 44% of episodes in this cohort, so most are missed. Fitting wasting and stunting doesn't validate trajectories or care, and severe wasting is visibly too low (4.8% vs 13.5%). Real benefit must be shown prospectively.
- **"The child gets no visit, newborns compete, you escalate to another overworked cadre?"** Infants under six months no longer compete; they go to the infant pathway (eval_v2). Undelivered work is blocked as a capacity exception owned by the supervisor, with no worker clock, and it's never escalated against the worker. It's still unmet need: the pilot must measure workload and completed care.
- **"A supervisor can claim an allowed purpose, or rewrite ledger and checkpoint together."** The gate refuses declared employment use; it can't stop misuse by someone with an allowed purpose. That needs minimisation, a contractual ban, worker participation and appeals (ADR 002). The decisions on screen 7 are recorded, not live; the checkpoint's external custody is simulated, and replacing both defeats it.
- **"Is any of this validated on real children?"** No. Only the state and district rates are real; child-level screens are simulations. Validation is the pilot.
