Episode = a cross of ≥ 1 bp beyond the pair's EWMA fair basis (C6 detector), from the first to the last exchange timestamp at which it is open. Kaplan–Meier with right-censoring at feed gaps (> 30 s). Greenwood CIs assume independent episodes; episodes cluster in time, so the **hour-block bootstrap CI** (200 resamples of the 24 hours) is the one to quote.

**Resolution caveat** (`eval/results/update_cadence.md`): in these samples Binance spot and OKX quotes are published on a 100 ms grid and Binance perpetual quotes every 26 ms, while Coinbase and Kraken update within milliseconds. A lifetime cannot be resolved below the cadence of the venues involved, so sub-26 ms decay is only measurable on Coinbase/Kraken/Deribit pairs.
