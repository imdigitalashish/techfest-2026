**Three numbers to remember:** ₹60,711 of parts per site · 57.4 vs 64.9 µg/m³ all-hours (simulated; no better in work hours) · construction = 2-5% of ambient PM10 in IIT Kanpur's study.

**Never say:** tamper-proof · "we will cut AQI by …" · "clean sites can keep working" (it's a proposal) · "no one else does this" · "field-validated".

**Fast answers** (full list: qa-prep.md)

- *Why you, in a crowded theme?* Others measure the city; we estimate which site added the dust — and say "inconclusive" when we can't tell.
- *Built or simulated?* Data pipeline, forecast benchmark, demo: built. Sites + sensors in the replay: simulated. Ring + misting: Round 2.
- *Sensors aren't accurate for dust.* Agreed (CSTEP 2025: qualitative). Side-by-side, reference co-location and a blind known-source test come first; DPCC verifies before fines.
- *Calm winds?* Below 0.5 m/s → "inconclusive". Site-level calm hasn't been measured yet; we'll report the abstention rate.
- *Only a few per cent of PM — why bother?* Because that source is restricted citywide; an evidence tool targets inspection. No city-AQI claim.
- *Trial during the restrictions?* Real site only before 1 Nov with approvals; otherwise an approved controlled test, reported as such.
