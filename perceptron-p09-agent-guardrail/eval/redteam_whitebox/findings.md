## Top 5 weaknesses

1. **Negation is not semantic.** The mention extractor treats words inside 'do not' clauses as positive authorization. Fix: Add clause-level negation scope and make negated provenance fail.
2. **Message text is barely grounded.** send_client_message does not require text/account provenance and relies on narrow regexes for harmful claims. Fix: Run provenance over message account/text plus semantic claim checks for returns, fees, status and privacy.
3. **Order type provenance is asymmetric.** Market orders are checked, but limit orders can be invented or substituted from quote data. Fix: Require user-confirmed order_type for both market and limit, and block market-to-limit conversion.
4. **Duplicate detection is not canonical.** Session-state symbol/price type variants bypass P-4.1. Fix: Normalize stored and proposed order keys before comparison.
5. **Parsing is global, not clause-scoped.** Compound buy/sell instructions and natural sell/cancel idioms lose valid provenance. Fix: Parse per instruction clause and resolve unique state references.
