Without synchronised randomness, the ghost wanders off.

Same seeds, same TWAP order. In naive twins, our first trade shifts every later random draw, so the ghost drifts into a different market and the "footprint" is mostly noise. Synchronised twins re-key every agent's random stream at each wake-up (world.py: _synchronise_rng), so the two markets differ only through reactions to our orders.
