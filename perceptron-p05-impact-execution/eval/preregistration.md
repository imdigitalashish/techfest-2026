## E8 — Attribution ablation: QP schedule vs front-loading (registered 2026-10-04 13:00 IST)
**Why:** a code review found that the "feedback" in IA_sched_only acts as a ~1.2× front-loading, not a brake (`eval/results/code-review-2026-10-04.md`). The confirmed E6 effect is therefore QP schedule + front-loading, unattributed.
- **Seeds:** validation 100–199 (n = 100), synced twins (`--rng-sync per_wakeup`), SELL 4,000 shares, 09:35–09:55, 40 slots. Same task as E5/E6.
- **Arms** (`eval/policies/e8_ablation.json`, generated by `eval/make_ablation_policies.py` from `eval/results/calibration.json`). All use market orders (`use_limit=false`):
  - TWAP.
  - QP_only: QP on, feedback off.
  - TWAP_legacy_fb: QP off, legacy feedback on.
  - IA_sched_only: QP on, legacy feedback on (the E6 policy).
  - IA_sched_fixed: QP on, corrected `pre_trade` feedback.
- **Primary contrasts** (paired by seed; mean ΔIS in bps with 95% bootstrap CI, 10k resamples, RandomState(0), seeds sorted):
  - A. QP main effect = ½[(QP_only − TWAP) + (IA_sched_only − TWAP_legacy_fb)].
  - B. Front-loading main effect = ½[(TWAP_legacy_fb − TWAP) + (IA_sched_only − QP_only)].
  - C. Interaction = (IA_sched_only − TWAP_legacy_fb) − (QP_only − TWAP).
  - D. Corrected-feedback increment = IA_sched_fixed − QP_only.
- **Attribution rule:**
  - Attribute the E6 effect to QP if A's CI excludes 0 and B's does not.
  - Attribute it to front-loading if B's CI excludes 0 and A's does not.
  - "Both" if both CIs exclude 0; "unattributed at n = 100" if neither does.
  - Same rule for the own-impact component.
  - No multiplicity correction: this is descriptive attribution on validation seeds, not a new confirmatory claim.
- **Command:** `nice -n 10 .venv/bin/python eval/run_experiment.py --exp E8_ablation_val --seeds 100:200 --qty 4000 --policies eval/policies/e8_ablation.json --workers 4 --rng-sync per_wakeup`.

**Outcome (appended 13:10 IST, after the run; registration text above unchanged):**
- **Attribution: IS → unattributed at this n; own impact → unattributed at this n** (`eval/results/E8_ablation_val_attribution.md`).
- Contrasts:
  - A (QP main): +0.0020 [−0.0252, +0.0295].
  - B (front-loading main): +0.0036 [−0.0188, +0.0256].
  - C (interaction): +0.0194 [−0.0175, +0.0548].
  - D (corrected feedback): −0.0122 [−0.0388, +0.0116].
- **The E6 policy IA_sched_only did not beat TWAP on these 100 validation seeds: ΔIS +0.0056 [−0.0284, +0.0393]** (E6, 200 test seeds: −0.0340 [−0.0563, −0.0127]). Its own-impact gain also vanished: +0.0005 [−0.0315, +0.0322].

## E7 — Realism scorecard: does ABIDES rmsc04 reproduce square-root impact? (registered 2026-10-04 13:00 IST)
- **Design:** TWAP SELL over 09:35–09:55 (40 slots), Q ∈ {1000, 2000, 4000, 8000, 16000} shares, validation seeds 100–129 (n = 30 per size, the same seeds at every size), synced twins.
- **Metrics:**
  - Peak impact I(Q) = mean twin gap at the end of execution (T1 = 1,200 s after 09:35), in cents (+ = adverse).
  - Participation Q/V, where V = mean null-twin volume traded during the window.
  - δ = OLS slope of log I(Q) on log(Q/V) over the 5 sizes, with a 95% CI from a paired bootstrap over seeds (resample seed indices jointly across sizes; 5k resamples).
  - Decay ratio = mean gap at T1 + 600 s / mean gap at T1.
- **Benchmarks:** the square-root law, δ ≈ 0.5. The Tokyo Stock Exchange full-account survey finds δ = 1/2 within errors (Sato & Kanazawa, PRL 135, 257401 (2025); arXiv:2411.13965). An ABM benchmark calibrated to the TSE gives ⟨δ⟩ = 0.489 (arXiv:2607.04280).
- **Decision rule (realism flag):**
  - δ's 95% CI overlaps [0.3, 0.7]: "consistent with concave, square-root-like impact".
  - δ CI above 0.7: "near-linear impact, not square-root-like" (flag in the abstract).
  - δ CI below 0.3, or I(Q) not increasing: "impact saturates or is unresolved" (flag).
- **Command:** `for q in 1000 2000 4000 8000 16000; do nice -n 10 .venv/bin/python eval/run_experiment.py --exp E7_size_q$q --seeds 100:130 --qty $q --policies eval/policies/twap_only.json --workers 4 --rng-sync per_wakeup; done; .venv/bin/python eval/realism.py --qs 1000 2000 4000 8000 16000`.

**Outcome (appended 13:20 IST; registration text above unchanged):**
- δ = 1.006, 95% paired-bootstrap CI [0.546, 1.760] (75% of resamples had positive impact at every size). **The verdict under the registered rule is "consistent with concave, square-root-like impact", but only because the rule accepts any CI that overlaps [0.3, 0.7].** The rule was too lenient; that is our mistake, reported rather than re-scored.
- Honest reading: the point estimate is near-linear. Post hoc, not pre-registered: impact grows ×2.00, ×2.34, ×1.85, ×1.82 per size doubling (square-root law: ×1.41), and the slope over the three resolved sizes is δ = 0.87.
- Ten minutes after completion the twin gap is ≤ 0 at every size, with every CI including 0: no detectable permanent impact. See `eval/results/realism_scorecard.md`.

## E9 — Synced calibration of the own-impact kernel (C4 isolated metric) (registered 2026-10-04 13:00 IST)
- **Design:** TWAP + PROBE (random slice sizes), train seeds 0–39, synced twins. Fit the exponential propagator (τ grid search; weighted least squares) on seeds 0–19; evaluate on seeds 20–39 with `eval/calibrate.py` (fit = first half, OOS = second half).
- **Metric:** out-of-sample R² of the seed-mean impact path over 0–1,200 s (`r2_mean_path`); secondary: per-seed R² and RMSE. Naive-twin baseline (E1): r2_mean_path = −4.19 (PROBE), −2.89 (TWAP).
- **Success criterion:** r2_mean_path > 0 for both TWAP and PROBE held-out paths, i.e. the kernel predicts our average footprint better than a constant.
- **Command:** `nice -n 10 .venv/bin/python eval/run_experiment.py --exp E9_calib_sync --seeds 0:40 --qty 4000 --policies eval/policies/calib.json --workers 4 --rng-sync per_wakeup && .venv/bin/python eval/calibrate.py --exp E9_calib_sync --out calibration_sync.json`.
- **Note:** `eval/policies/test.json` and `confirm.json` keep using the naive-twin calibration (`calibration.json`) so E3–E6 stay reproducible; the synced kernel is reported, not swapped in.

**Outcome (appended 13:21 IST):** **success criterion NOT met.**
- r2_mean_path: TWAP −0.02, PROBE −0.41 (naive E1: −2.89 / −4.19).
- Per-seed RMSE: 4.24¢ / 4.06¢ (naive: 19.85¢ / 19.42¢), about 4.7× lower.
- Synced kernel: τ = 180 s, g_tr = 0.0067¢/share, g_perm ≈ −0.0003 (≈ 0, consistent with E7's fully transient impact).
- The exponential propagator does not yet predict our average footprint better than a constant. See `eval/results/calibration_sync.json`.

## E10: Replication and attribution at n = 400 on fresh test seeds (registered 2026-10-04 13:12 IST)
**Why:** H1 passed on test seeds 1060–1259 (−0.034 bps), but E8 (val 100–199) found +0.006. Either the effect is smaller than E6 estimated, or it varies by seed set. A larger fresh sample settles both the replication and the attribution.
- **Seeds:** fresh test seeds 1260–1659 (n = 400; never used before). Synced twins; the same task as E5–E8.
- **Arms:** identical to E8 (`eval/policies/e8_ablation.json`): TWAP, QP_only, TWAP_legacy_fb, IA_sched_only, IA_sched_fixed.
- **H2 (replication of H1, confirmatory):** IA_sched_only has mean paired ΔIS vs TWAP < 0. One-sided α = 0.05: the 95th percentile of the bootstrap mean (10k resamples, RandomState(0), seeds sorted) is < 0.
- **Attribution:** contrasts A–D and the attribution rule exactly as in E8, computed with `eval/attribution.py --exp E10_replication_test`.
- **Reporting rule:**
  - The abstract reports H2's result whichever way it goes.
  - If H2 fails, the abstract must not claim that the agent beats TWAP on total slippage. It will instead report the pooled estimate over all synced test seeds (E5 ∪ E6 ∪ E10; descriptive, not confirmatory) and the robust components.
- **Command:** `nice -n 10 .venv/bin/python eval/run_experiment.py --exp E10_replication_test --seeds 1260:1660 --qty 4000 --policies eval/policies/e8_ablation.json --workers 4 --rng-sync per_wakeup`.
