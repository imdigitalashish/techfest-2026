## 4. Five changes most likely to earn first place by 31 Oct

1. **Deliver the owned-space proof first, by 7 Oct.** A timed two/three-room phone capture -> untouched pipeline outputs -> anonymous HTTPS walkthrough, with permission-cleared sample input and committed provenance/report. Include one awkward doorway and one low-texture/reflective area. This closes the largest compliance and credibility gap; another public-scene ablation does not.
2. **Make fidelity inspectable, by 12 Oct.** Put same-pose home photo/raw-render/export-render comparisons beside room coverage, independent taped dimensions and measured timing. Verify exported SH/pruning losses. Re-shoot weak coverage rather than replacing evidence with flattering viewpoints. A smooth, recognizable home beats an elaborate architecture inventory.
3. **Win the actual delivery experience, by 20 Oct.** Use compression/progressive loading plus a measured render budget, not unqualified splat-count reduction. Target the existing <=5 s meaningful visibility and <=20 ms walking p95 with a fixed, room-covering trace, three cold runs and a normal laptop. Publish raw/export fidelity alongside performance; show a quality-preserving before/after.
4. **Prove a repeatable field procedure, by 24 Oct.** Have a second person follow the SOP without coaching. Record capture, transfer, QA, retries, compute and deploy separately; demonstrate detecting and recovering one failed clip. Gate missing rooms/scale, fix cache provenance, and use independent validation distances. This makes D' a demonstrated contribution rather than a formula.
5. **Freeze a judge-proof package by 28 Oct; submit before 31 Oct.** Actual <=5-page PDF, 4:30 home demo, owned sample/setup, licence/contribution notes and one master links Doc in an anonymously accessible Drive folder. Open on the result, show authentic capture and sustained free roaming, keep R2 to a brief clearly labelled preview. Remove every pending field and absolute claim that evidence cannot support; verify registration by 15 Oct.

These are leverage-ranked recommendations, not a prediction against unseen competitors. Defer AR, automatic semantic models and major posing-engine swaps until the four R1 criteria are convincingly demonstrated.
