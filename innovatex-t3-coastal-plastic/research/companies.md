### The Ocean Cleanup — Interceptor family + 30 Cities Program (non-profit, NL)
- **Mumbai is a 2026 target city.** Press release "The Ocean Cleanup Enters India; To Tackle Mumbai's 5 Million kg Plastic Flow", Mumbai, 10 Feb 2026: Mumbai is "among 30 cities worldwide to cut one-third of global river-based plastic emissions"; "Trombay and Malad waterways identified as initial priority sites; deployment is planned for 2026"; their 'Smart Rivers Survey' used "drones, AI-enhanced remote-sensing cameras, GPS drifters, and global data registries"; "the metropolitan region releases an estimated 5 million kilograms of plastic waste into the Arabian Sea and Indian Ocean every year"; "these initial systems are expected to recover 61 to 92 tonnes of plastic per year" *(claim/forecast)*. → **This is the source of the "5 million kg" figure in our brief.** https://theoceancleanup.com/press/press-releases/the-ocean-cleanup-enters-india/
- **Permits + MoU.** Press release "The Ocean Cleanup moving towards deployment of Mumbai Interceptors", Mumbai, 30 Apr 2026: MoU signed with the Maharashtra Pollution Control Board (MPCB); "secured permits from Municipal Corporation of Greater Mumbai (MCGM), Storm Water Drain Department"; pilots "in Trombay Creek and Malad Creek"; "On-ground operations and waste management will be led by social enterprise Plastic Fischer, which, with more than 100 employees, currently operates across seven Indian cities"; implementation also with United Way Mumbai. https://theoceancleanup.com/press/press-releases/the-ocean-cleanup-deploys-mumbai-interceptors/
- **Programme scale.** Update "Funding unlocked to help stop one-third of river pollution" (published 9 Mar 2026): The Audacious Project committed "$121 million of the $350 million needed"; 2026 planned Interceptor deployments listed include Barranquilla, Santo Domingo, **Mumbai**, Jakarta, Denpasar, Panama City; organisation states it has "prevent[ed] nearly 50 million kilograms of trash from reaching the ocean" and intercepts "an estimated 2-5 percent of global river-borne plastic emissions" *(claim)*. https://theoceancleanup.com/updates/funding-unlocked-for-the-30-cities-program/
- **Rivers page:** "just 1000 of the three million rivers ... are responsible for 80% of riverine pollution"; Kingston (Jamaica) "was the first to feature multiple Interceptors in one city"; Interceptor 006 (Las Vacas, Guatemala) "our highest performing to date". https://theoceancleanup.com/rivers/
- **Implication for us:** the two outfalls named in the brief (Malad, Trombay) are already being taken by the best-funded player in the field. Our pitch should not be "another barrier at Malad"; it should be the *measurement + dispatch + MRV layer* that (a) works at the hundreds of smaller nullah outfalls a 61–92 t/yr Interceptor programme does not reach, and (b) could plug into Interceptor/Plastic Fischer sites as an independent verifier.
