## TL;DR
1. **The PWM Rules 2016 put "no littering" and segregation duties on generators, and assign duties to local bodies.** EPR covers both pre- and post-consumer plastic packaging. [P1]
2. **EPR categories:** I rigid; II flexible, single- or multi-layer, explicitly including "carry bags, plastic sachet or pouches"; III multilayered with at least one non-plastic layer; IV compostable; V biodegradable. **Our target stream (films, carry bags, sachets) is Category II/III**, the hardest to recycle. [P1]
3. **Every EPR actor must register on CPCB's centralised portal** and "shall not deal with any entity not registered". An interception operator therefore needs a registered processor (PWP) partner, or must register itself, to turn catch into EPR value. [P1]
4. **End-of-life routes are allowed only for non-recyclable plastic:** "road construction, waste to energy, waste to oil, cement kilns (for co processing)". This is the realistic route for wet, dirty film and MLP from drains. [P1]
5. **Whether drain- or river-recovered plastic earns EPR certificates: NOT FOUND.** The consolidated text shows no explicit drain/river/beach provision. Present it as an open eligibility question with a registered-PWP workaround, not as guaranteed revenue. [P1]
6. **Voluntary credits:** an OBP Credit is 1 kg of low-value ocean-bound plastic "removed from the environment … and adequately treated by a certified organization", with no forecast credits. Verra issues plastic credits only after collection is quantified, verified by a VVB and approved by Verra. Both require auditable MRV, so **MRV is a product in itself**. [P2][P3]
7. **BMC has money:** the 2026-27 budget is ₹80,952.56 crore (+8.7 %) with ₹48,164.26 crore capex, and roads plus storm-water drains take "more than half". The SWM department has ₹580.82 crore for 2026-27 project works (₹364.62 crore in RE 2025-26). [P4][P6][P7]
8. **BMC loses money on unverifiable quantities:** the Mithi desilting FIR cites a ₹65.54 crore wrongful loss, and ₹1,100 crore was spent on the Mithi by BMC and MMRDA since 2005. Verified-tonnage contracts are the selling point. [P5]
9. **Labour (Maharashtra, Zone 1, 1 Jul–31 Dec 2025):** full-time sweeper/scavenger minimum wage = ₹10,000 basic + ₹6,834 special allowance = **₹16,834/month**. Unskilled shops & establishments = **₹13,635/month**. [P8]
10. **Benchmark:** Deonar legacy-waste bioremediation of 185 lakh t costs ₹3,035.55 crore, ≈ **₹1,641/t** (DERIVED). This gives a reference for what BMC pays per tonne to deal with waste. [P6]

## 1. PWM Rules 2016 (as amended; consolidated 14 Mar 2024) and EPR
- Generators: QUOTE "mandate the generators of plastic waste to take steps to minimize generation of plastic waste, not to litter the plastic waste, ensure segregated storage of waste at source and hand over segregated waste … The rules also mandate the responsibilities of local bodies, gram panchayats, waste generators, retailers and street vendors". [P1]
- Scope: QUOTE "Extended Producer Responsibility shall be applicable to both pre-consumer and post-consumer plastic packaging waste." Covered entities: producers, importers and brand owners of plastic packaging; Plastic Waste Processors; raw-material manufacturers/importers; makers of compostable/biodegradable items. [P1]
- Categories (exact): "Category II Flexible plastic packaging of single layer or multilayer (more than one layer with different types of plastic), plastic sheets or like and covers made of plastic sheet, carry bags, plastic sachet or pouches"; "Category III Multilayered plastic packaging (at least one layer of plastic and at least one layer of material other than plastic)". [P1]
- Portal: QUOTE "shall not carry any business without registration obtained through online centralized portal developed by Central Pollution Control Board." [P1]
- Targets: QUOTE "Extended Producer Responsibility target … 2021 - 22 | 25% … 2022 - 23 | 70% … 2023 - 24 | 100%". [P1]
- End of life: QUOTE "Only those plastics, which cannot be recycled will be sent for end of life disposal such as road construction, waste to energy, waste to oil, cement kilns (for co processing) etc." [P1]
- **NOT FOUND (workers died):** amendment-by-amendment changes (2018/2021/2022/2024/2025), carry-bag thickness dates, EC (environmental compensation) rates, and the fake-certificate scandal.

## 2. Voluntary plastic credits
- OBP: QUOTE "Each OBP Credit is equivalent to 1Kg of low value OBP that has been removed from the environment in most needed locations and adequately treated by a certified organization." / "does not allow forecasted or estimated plastic credits." [P2]
- Verra PWRS: QUOTE "Project activities need to be implemented and monitored and the plastic waste collected and/or recycled needs to be quantified; 2. A validation/verification body (VVB) to verify … 3. Verra needs to approve the verification." [P3]
- **NOT FOUND:** credit prices (USD/t), Indian projects, and the criticisms (GAIA/BFFP etc.). Do not quote prices until they are sourced.

## 3. Maharashtra ban and BMC enforcement
- The ban notification is dated 23 March 2018 and covers "production, use, sale, transportation, handling, and storage"; "Despite this ban, the use of plastic products in the city remains rampant." [P11]
- 2024: "BMC collected INR 41.7 lakh in penalties by acting against 44,448 establishments and confiscating 2,148 kilograms of plastic". 2025 to 20 Jan: "167 kilograms … fines amounting to INR 6.1 lakh". [P9]
- Cumulative, Shops & Establishments department (Marathi source): 9,53,734 visits, 1,01,278 kg seized, ₹7,64,40,000 fines, 15,248 cases. [P10]
- **UNVERIFIED:** fine slabs (₹5,000 / ₹10,000 / ₹25,000 + imprisonment) and PET/milk-pouch buy-back deposits.

## 4. BMC budget, desilting and waste benchmarks
- QUOTE "allocation of a whopping Rs. 80,952.56 Cr. This marks an increase of 8.7%"; "allocates Rs. 48,164.26 Crore as capital expenditure"; "General budget which includes roads and storm-water drains takes up more than half of the allocation." (2026-27) [P4]
- SWM: QUOTE "a provision of INR 364.62 crore has been made in the revised estimates for 2025–26. Additionally, INR 580.82 crore has been allocated in the 2026–27 financial year". Corroborated by [P7]. [P6]
