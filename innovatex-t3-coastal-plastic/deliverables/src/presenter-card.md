**Fast answers**

- *The Ocean Cleanup is here?* It covers two creek mouths, {{f:F02|61 to 92 tonnes}} a year expected. We propose to make BMC's booms measurable.
- *Simulated?* Yes, and it only chooses our first experiments. Real rain, archived forecasts and fitted tide; plastic behaviour is assumed.
- *Flooding?* The release is designed to open at a set head. Opening head, delay and reset are what test T3 measures. We don't claim it can't fail.
- *Novel trap?* No; floating tidal traps exist (Bandalong). What we propose is a retrofit of BMC's booms plus timing and records.
- *Camera at night?* Then fill is "unknown" and crews follow the roster. The ultrasonic measures water level, not fill.
- *Who pays?* The existing boom maintenance budget. EPR is upside: drain-plastic eligibility is not settled.

**Don't say:** "tamper-proof" · "blockchain" · "can't flood" · "seasons" (say "parameter settings") · "the booms have no sensors" · absolute tonnes from the twin · "our trap is new" · any guilt in the Mithi case · "we built the hardware" (it's PLANNED).
