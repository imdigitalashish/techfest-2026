**Fast answers**

- *The Ocean Cleanup is here already?* Two creek mouths, {{f:F02|61 to 92 tonnes}} a year expected. We serve BMC's other booms.
- *Simulated?* Trust the ranking, not tonnes. Real rain, archived forecasts and fitted tide; 2024–25 held out; the circularity is stated.
- *Flooding?* The fuse opens at a set head; the pocket keeps its catch. We'll show the measured head-rise envelope.
- *Novel trap?* No. Floating tidal traps exist (Bandalong). Our contribution is the retrofit, the fuse, and the operations and evidence.
- *Camera at night?* Primary reading is range-sensor fill; the camera supports it.
- *Who pays?* BMC's existing boom budget; EPR is upside, because drain-plastic eligibility is not settled.
