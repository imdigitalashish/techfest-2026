## Raw review (verbatim)

1. **Major / Non-Blocking — The portfolio undervalues manufacturing decisions.** Evidence: ADR candidate scores and synthesis; official Challenge 2 deliverables and rubrics. My scores are comparative judgments, not predicted jury marks: 1 weak–5 strong, with feasibility assessed for a two-person core, December delivery and the exam blackout.

| Candidate | Impact R1/R2 | Novelty | Feasibility | Measurability | Demoability |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| A: verified-provenance ingestion | 4/5 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| B: regional/method/prospective grid factors | 3/3 | 2 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| C: calibrated uncertainty/data-gap ranking | 4/4 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 3 |
| D: one-component BOM/Indian routes | 5/5 | 3 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| E: LLM copilot | 2/2 | 1 | 5 | 2 | 4 |

   Round 1 assigns 70 points to understanding, soundness and roadmap; Round 2 assigns 65 to robustness, integration and usability. Novelty cannot compensate for weak methodological foundations. **Recommendation:** Prefer A+D as the headline pair, with bounded B underneath and harmonization mandatory. Demonstrate an equivalent component using Indian steel routes or primary/recycled aluminium; distinguish battery-cell origin from Indian pack assembly. This directly supports manufacturer decisions. Require a source-availability gate: corporate BRSR ratios are not automatically product-level LCI factors.

2. **Critical / Blocking — A verifies text presence, not a defensible factor.** Evidence: `verifier.py` `quote_found`; architecture C3 error guarantees. The verifier receives neither the record's value/unit nor its page locator. Changing a field while retaining its quote cannot be detected. Read-only tests also confirmed that `0.675` matches inside `10.675`, and ordered fragments can join different pages' years and values. **Recommendation:** Separate source-anchored, semantically checked, and reviewer-approved states. Bind values to table headers, units, years, reference products and page/cell coordinates, with source hashes. Until then, call this an evidence-presence check—not "verified provenance" guaranteeing factual correctness.

3. **Major / Blocking — The ratchet rewards an increasingly familiar benchmark.** Evidence: ratchet ledger (v3 failure, v4 repair); evaluator gold selection and perturbation. The v3 failure is valuable evidence: 71/71 quotes found nevertheless produced three false accepts. Removing the fallback was sensible, but v4's repaired evaluation is no longer untouched validation. Thirty-five records from one annex are not thirty-five independent document tests. Even 0/142 independent negatives would imply an approximately 2.1% one-sided 95% upper false-accept bound, not zero; these synthetic, clustered negatives do not justify even that real-world interpretation. **Recommendation:** Freeze document/publisher-level development and untouched test sets. Score document-macro F1, complete-record precision/recall, abstention, and reviewer minutes per correct usable record. Include inaccessible/failed documents in coverage. Add unchanged-quote/wrong-field, neighbouring-cell, unit, year and scope negatives. Keep "zero observed accepts" as a regression requirement, not a statistical guarantee.

4. **Major / Blocking — B's metrics do not validate its advertised geography.** Evidence: `grid.py`; ADR consequences. National generation plus state T&D losses is not state consumption carbon intensity. Grid integration does not imply identical state supply mixes; commercial losses further complicate interpretation. Every state allocation could be wrong while the national aggregate matches exactly. Reproducing 1,424.19 BU verifies generation aggregation, not an emission factor. **Recommendation:** Deliver a national attributional baseline first. Reconcile emissions and generation separately, including renewables and net/gross treatment; distinguish generation-based from consumption-based products. Forecast claims require vintage-frozen, rolling-origin back-tests against persistence, reporting MAE and signed bias—not one already-observed year. Treat national reconstruction as an accounting test, not independent validation.

5. **Critical / Blocking — The proposed CO2e build-up contains a dimensional error.** Evidence: upstream note (proposed derivation and gaps); upstream factors (stationary combustion, specific coal consumption). Given the declared units, TJ/kWh multiplied by kg gas/TJ and kg CO2e/kg gas already yields kg CO2e/kWh. The additional `/1000` suppresses stationary non-CO2 emissions one thousandfold. This concerns the proposed derivation, not the committed v2 results. The 0.666 kg coal/kWh fallback is gross coal-generation consumption, not national net-generation consumption. **Recommendation:** Add dimensional tests, fuel-generation weighting, auxiliary-consumption and GCV/NCV conversions, and domestic/imported-coal treatment. Bound missing upstream contributions explicitly. Adding AR6 grid records cannot harmonize legacy GWP20 or unspecified manufacturing factors by relabelling their aggregate CO2e.

6. **Major / Blocking — "Grid method dominates" confuses contribution, scenarios and uncertainty.** Evidence: landscape §5; harness-v2 results. Current scooter savings are 39.5% average, 18.2% OM and 56.4% BM; the sedan changes from 11.9% saving to 17.8% increase under OM. These demonstrate conditional sensitivity, not general statistical dominance. Average/OM/BM answer different questions; OM is neither a guaranteed upper bound nor a charging-time marginal model. **Recommendation:** Separate contribution analysis, within-method parameter uncertainty and between-method scenarios. Test consumption, lifetime, replacement, cell origin and materials under defensible joint assumptions. Ten thousand draws improve numerical precision, not empirical calibration. Harmonization deserves more attention than a state map; "calibrated uncertainty" requires compatible independent benchmarks, coverage and interval-width/proper-score evaluation. Five heterogeneous studies are insufficient grounds for a strong calibration claim.

7. **Major / Blocking — Novelty is overstated against established tools and recent literature.** Evidence: ADR novelty scores; landscape §§3–5. openLCA/Brightway already provide documented inventories and uncertainty machinery; ecoinvent/GaBi provide geographically differentiated inventories. Carculator supports country selection and prospective comparisons; premise explicitly maps India. European defaults are not European-only capability. Confirmed literature includes Kumar et al. (2025), *A Large Language Model-based Framework to Retrieve Life Cycle Inventory and Environmental Impact Data from Scientific Literature* (doi:10.1021/acs.est.5c05955), and Tang et al. (2026), *From literature to knowledge graphs: automated extraction and retrieval of life cycle assessment data with large language models* (doi:10.1007/s44498-026-00135-8). Exact quote-validation equivalence remains unverified. **Recommendation:** Claim an openly auditable Indian corpus, compatibility checks and demonstrated curation-effort reduction—not first-ever extraction, provenance or prospective India LCA. A narrow GitHub search cannot establish absence.

8. **Critical / Blocking — Biggest risk: auditable-looking numbers that are not comparable.** Evidence: architecture C3/C7 status; earlier jury review issues 1, 12 and 13. Next two weeks: 4–7 October: freeze one decision, functional equivalence, system boundary and characterization method; audit whether D has genuinely compatible public sources. 8–12 October: independently annotate a diverse document set, reserve unseen documents, benchmark extraction against manual/rule baselines, and test the upstream dimensional corrections. 13–18 October: demonstrate one complete source-to-comparison chain, including deliberate rejection of an incompatible factor; obtain an independent technical review. For 30 October, clearly distinguish demonstrated capability from December commitments. Plan no exam-week delivery, establish an integration checkpoint before 19 November, and freeze December features early enough for offline rehearsals. If D's data gate fails, retain harmonized national B rather than inventing supplier detail.

**Five-line verdict.** A — MODIFY: keep ingestion, but make semantic qualification and measured reviewer effort—not quote presence—the innovation. B — MODIFY: retain a harmonized national-grid foundation; defer state/prospective expansion until independently testable. C — MERGE: make harmonization and honest uncertainty mandatory, without premature calibration claims. D — REPLACE B's headline slot: a source-qualified, one-component Indian route/origin comparison better demonstrates manufacturer utility. E — KEEP REJECTED: a chat copilot adds little judging value relative to its reliability burden.
