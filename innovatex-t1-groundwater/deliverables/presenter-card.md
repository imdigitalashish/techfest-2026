**Demo cues (45 s):**

1. Left panel: "185 stations, only 46 reported this year, a quarter of readings exactly zero."
2. Press **space**: "Simulated pit, Delhi's real 2025 rain. Every storm gets a verdict."
3. At the red band: "A rise the rain can't explain: random inspection, not a penalty."
4. At the end: "Drain rate below half: a maintenance ticket."

**Five hardest questions:**

1. *"A level sensor isn't recharge."* Right. We report the drain rate (the core: maintenance screening) and a model-based infiltrated-volume estimate. A leak out of the pit looks like infiltration, so suspicious cases go to inspection. A per-event band is planned.
2. *"3 % grades your own simulator."* Yes, so we lead with 13 % (14.5 % with a realistic probe), over 1,233 scored event windows. The real number comes from blind weighed-water tests in November.
3. *"Who pays if the owner can lose the rebate?"* Not households first. Our prospective pilot customer is DJB's restoration programme (59 empanelled providers); no contact yet, that's our ask. Inspectors override; no bill changes automatically.
4. *"Can an owner cheat?"* Yes: a bypass during real rain, or a small or slow pour. A 20 L bucket isn't even an event. Large sudden pulses the rain can't explain are flagged (10/10 simulated, 500–2,000 L) → random inspection.
5. *"Battery life?"* Not claimed until measured. Probe settle time decides D-cell vs solar; we measure it in October.
