**Opening line (0:00):** *"Delhi has made rainwater harvesting compulsory for 25 years and paid crores in rebates for it. Nobody can tell you whether those pits work. We built the instrument that can."*

**Say these three numbers, exactly this way:**

1. **13 %**: median per-storm error on our stress test (simulation). *"3 % is only when our models agree, so we don't sell that."*
2. **₹11,166 per node** in verified retail parts, vs ≈ ₹38,000 for one CGWA-compliant logger (Acorn) that logs twice a day.
3. **733 million litres a year**: the campaign's *estimate* (Indian Express). *"That's the number we'd audit."*

**Never say:** "aquifer recharge" · "self-certification is the rule" (it's *planned*) · "never checked" · a battery life · "we detect illegal borewells".

**Demo cues (45 s):**

1. Left panel: "185 stations, only 46 reported this year, a quarter of readings exactly zero."
2. Press **space**: "Simulated pit, Delhi's real 2025 rain. Every storm gets a verdict."
3. At the red band: "A rise the rain can't explain: random inspection, not a penalty."
4. At the end: "Drain rate below half: a maintenance ticket."

**Five hardest questions:**

1. *"A level sensor isn't recharge."* Right. We report drain rate and infiltrated volume ± band at the structure, never aquifer recharge.
2. *"3 % grades your own simulator."* Yes, so we lead with 13 % (14.5 % with a realistic probe). The real number comes from blind weighed-water tests by 1 Dec.
3. *"Who pays if the owner can lose the rebate?"* Not households first: DJB's government-building restorations via its 59 empanelled providers. Inspectors override; no bill changes automatically.
4. *"Can an owner cheat?"* A bypass during real rain can. A pour without rain is flagged (10/10 simulated), and that triggers a random inspection.
5. *"Battery life?"* Not claimed until measured. Probe settle time decides D-cell vs solar; we measure it in October.
