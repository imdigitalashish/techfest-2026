## 2. Rainwater Harvesting (RWH) mandate in Delhi

**Plot-size threshold:** RWH is mandatory on all plots/buildings (residential, commercial, institutional) of **100 sq m or more** in Delhi. [Hindustan Times, 21 Apr 2026]

**Financial assistance scheme (DJB subsidy for installation):**
- Plots 100–499.99 sqm: subsidy up to **50% of cost or ₹25,000, whichever is lower**
- Plots ≥500 sqm: subsidy cap of **₹50,000**
(Source: Hindustan Times, "Delhi makes rainwater harvesting mandatory; penalties for violators," 21 Apr 2026, quoting water minister Parvesh Verma's Delhi Secretariat meeting)

**Rebate for functional RWH:**
- **10% rebate** on DJB water bill if RWH system is functional
- **15% rebate** if BOTH rainwater harvesting AND wastewater recycling systems are installed and functional
- Rebate can be **withdrawn** if inspection finds the system non-functional/not maintained

**Penalty for non-compliance:**
- **1.5× increase in water bill** for non-compliance
- **Disconnection of water supply** in persistent/severe non-compliance cases
- For government buildings specifically: an initial **10% cut**, escalating to full disconnection if lapses continue

**Monitoring/enforcement mechanism (as of Apr 2026 announcement):**
- A **self-disclosure mechanism** is being introduced: property owners must **annually certify** (self-declare) that their RWH system is functional
- DJB issues "adequacy and functionality certificates" after inspection, requiring **periodic renewal**
- Quote: "Till now, there was a rule but no real checking. That will change. We will introduce a system of verification and accountability so that this is implemented on the ground, not just on paper" — Water Minister Parvesh Verma
- DJB is the **nodal agency** for implementation, monitoring, financial support
- Enforcement drive covers 60+ Delhi govt/Centre departments; target: functional RWH in government buildings, parks, residential colonies, institutional campuses **before monsoon 2026**

**Non-functional/clogged RWH — compliance numbers (government-sector audit, as of Apr 2026):**
- **₹204.46 crore** in RWH rebates disbursed by DJB **2017–2024** (cumulative)
- Schools/colleges: RWH installed in **4,343 out of 4,861** identified sites (89.3%); deadline **30 June 2026** for remaining sites
- Other government departments: **2,564 out of 3,598** identified sites completed (71.3%) — implies ~1,034 government-site RWH systems still NOT installed as of this report
- This directly evidences the "non-functional/incomplete RWH" pain point: roughly **28.7% of non-school govt sites** and **10.7% of school/college sites** lacked installed RWH as of the April 2026 enforcement push, even though the mandate dates to a 2001 Delhi RWH policy / reinforced by a 2020s circular.

**Context/quote (local pain point):** "For four months every year, rainwater flows through our drains and goes to waste. If we channel this water into the ground, we can recharge our groundwater and reduce the crisis we face every summer." — Parvesh Verma, Delhi Water Minister [HT, 21 Apr 2026]

Source: Hindustan Times, "Delhi makes rainwater harvesting mandatory; penalties for violators," published 2026-04-21T04:22:17+05:30, URL: https://www.hindustantimes.com/cities/delhi-news/delhi-makes-rainwater-harvesting-mandatory-penalties-for-violators-101776711439024.html (opened and quoted directly, full text retrieved)

**Older DJB circular (dated 10.07.2020)** — PDF at https://delhijalboard.delhi.gov.in/sites/default/files/Jalboard/generic_multiple_files/circular_10.07.2020.pdf — confirms RWH circular predates 2026 announcement; this PDF is image/scan-based (JPEG-encoded pages), text extraction via raw fetch was not feasible in markdown mode. ## 3. NGT and court orders 2019–2026 on illegal groundwater extraction in Delhi

### 3.1 Headline statewide numbers (via NGT proceedings, reported by Hindustan Times)
Per Hindustan Times (published 2025-06-10T05:36:12+05:30), reporting on an NGT order dated **30 May 2025** (joint committee case re: Manakpura/Karol Bagh, central Delhi):
> "Last month, district magistrates in Delhi informed NGT that **15,962 illegal borewells had been sealed**, but **more than 4,000 identified by DJB couldn't be traced on the ground**. Earlier, **20,297 illegal borewells were identified by DJB** that were impacting Delhi's groundwater table."

This gives a clean, quotable, judge-friendly local stat set:
- **20,297** illegal borewells identified by DJB (cumulative, as of ~April/May 2025)
- **15,962** sealed (≈78.6% of identified)
- **4,000+** identified-but-untraceable on the ground (≈20% "ghost" non-compliance — i.e., enforcement cannot even locate ~1 in 5 flagged illegal borewells)
- Implies a residual gap of roughly **4,335** (20,297 − 15,962) unresolved/untraced/unsealed at that date.

Source: Hindustan Times, "NGT seals illegal borewell in central Delhi," 2025-06-10, https://www.hindustantimes.com/cities/delhi-news/ngt-seals-illegal-borewell-in-central-delhi-101749492629182.html — case detail: joint committee (CPCB member secretary + DJB CEO + DM Central) constituted by NGT in March 2025, acting on a petition by resident Naresh Kumar alleging a "water supply mafia" in Manakpura near Karol Bagh. Order dated 30 May 2025 by NGT judicial member Arun Kumar Tyagi and expert member Dr Afroz Ahmad. Quote from the original petition: "there is an aqua problem of depleting groundwater in our area and this is happening due to illegal extraction of ground water by water supply mafia... groundwater is being extracted at brisk pace and in huge quantity for the last about more than 7-8 months." Borewell sealed, electricity disconnected; final Environmental Compensation (EC) amount held pending Supreme Court/Allahabad HC rulings on state PCB competence to levy EC; DPCC directed not to impose EC till further orders.

### 3.2 Deepak Singh vs Central Ground Water Authority of India — OA 703/2022 (NGT Principal Bench)
Order dated **27 July 2023** (Coram: Justice Sheo Kumar Singh, Chairperson; Justice Arun Kumar Tyagi, Judicial Member; Dr A. Senthil Vel, Expert Member). Full order text opened at https://indiankanoon.org/doc/19049265/ (IndianKanoon reproduction of NGT order).

Key facts/orders:
- Issue: violation of the **Water (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act, 1974** — illegal groundwater extraction by respondents 6–16 "for the purpose of construction, etc. without obtaining any requisite permission from the competent authority" in Shahdara.
- DJB had sent letters (14.06.2022, 07.07.2022) to respondents for action; **SDM concerned took no action** — prompting NGT intervention.
- **24 March 2023 order**: NGT constituted a **Committee comprising SDM Shahdara + DJB officers + CGWA officers** "for periodical monitoring and taking of preventative and remedial measures," reporting to the Tribunal within 3 months (as searchable/OCR PDF, not image PDF).
- DPCC compliance report cites a **Chief Secretary Delhi SOP (12 June 2020)**: "Regulation of extraction of ground water, [closure], prohibition of illegal activities relating to use of borewells/tubewells":
  - (a) Drawing groundwater via borewell/tubewell for domestic, commercial, agricultural or industrial use **without prior permission of the "Competent Authority" is illegal**.
  - (b) **Deputy Commissioner (Revenue)** supervises detection of illegal wells and closure.
  - (c) **Joint action teams** under the SDM combine **DJB + DISCOMs (power utilities) + Local Police** to close illegal borewells (power disconnection is part of the enforcement toolkit).
  - (d) Deputy Commissioner (chair, Advisory Committee) forwards illegal-borewell details to **DPCC for Environmental Compensation (EC)**.
  - (e) DPCC assesses EC per **CPCB methodology (report dated 26.06.2019)**; non-recovery is pursued as **arrears of land revenue**.
- Outcome in this case: DJB confirmed (letter 21.12.2022) that borewells of **respondents 6,7,8,9,10,11,13,14 & 15 were sealed** per SDM Shahdara order; **respondent 12's premises were locked** (inspection blocked); **respondent 16's borewell was found still working** (non-compliant).
- DPCC issued show-cause notices for EC of **₹30,000 each** against respondents 6,7,8,9,10,11,13,14,15 (03.01.2023) for illegal groundwater extraction.
- NGT's order is strongly worded against **SDM inaction**: "When the law protector becomes the law violators, how law will be protected... The conduct shown in the present proceedings are picture of fragrant [sic] violation of law and rules made by the state by the executive functionaries."

### 3.3 Sh. Rishi Jindal v. Delhi Pollution Control Committee & Ors. — improper sealing case
Execution application before NGT Principal Bench (Bench: Justice Prakash Shrivastava, Chairperson; Dr Afroz Ahmad, Expert Member), seeking compliance with an underlying order of **28 January 2025** re: illegal borewell operation in Delhi. Reported by The Bar Bulletin (legal news outlet), https://thebarbulletin.com/ngt-delhi-illegal-borewells-improper-sealing-report/ — article undated in visible text but references a listing date of **01-04-2026** (i.e., reported shortly before that).

Key facts:
- SDMs of **Civil Lines, Sarswati Vihar, and Model Town** submitted that certain borewells had been sealed.
- NGT found from photographic evidence that **sealing was improper** — "the borewells could be operated very conveniently without removing the seal" (i.e., cosmetic/non-physical sealing, a direct enforcement-quality gap).
- SDM Model Town committed to removing the submersible pump and **sealing with concrete** within two weeks; directed to file compliance report within three weeks.
- Matter next listed **01 April 2026**.

### 3.4 Mohd. Danish v. Government of NCT of Delhi & Ors. — OA 589/2024 → MA 11/2026 — EC funds redirected to lake/wetland rejuvenation
Reported by LawNotify (legal news aggregator), dated **17 February 2026**: https://lawnotify.in/ngt-directs-dpcc-to-use-groundwater-penalty-for-delhi-water-body-rejuvenation/ — order passed by NGT bench (Justice Arun Kumar Tyagi, Dr Afroz Ahmad) on **16 February 2026**.

Case timeline (fully quotable, concrete, dated):
- **October 2023**: Letter petition by applicant Mohd Danish alleging illegal groundwater extraction for construction at **D-23, Gali No. 16, Chauhan Banger, Brahampuri** (northeast Delhi).
- **July 2024**: NGT disposed of the original application (OA 589/2024), directing a joint committee to inspect and act.
- **6 September 2024**: Joint inspection by DPCC + SDM Seelampur — found a borewell installed "seven to eight years ago" with **no extraction permission**.
- **9 September 2024**: SDM Seelampur **sealed** the borewell (corrigendum later issued for a clerical error in the sealing memo).
- **March 2025**: DPCC issued show-cause notice proposing EC of **₹35,064**.
- **May 2025**: EC order confirmed after owner's waiver request rejected.
- **October 2025**: Non-payment → recovery letter sent to SDM to recover as arrears of land revenue.
- **10 November 2025**: EC amount **deposited via demand draft**.
- **16 February 2026**: NGT directed DPCC to prepare, **in consultation with the District Wetland Committee/State Wetland Authority**, an action plan to use this recovered EC money for **rejuvenation of water bodies across NCT Delhi**, to be implemented within **6 months**, with compliance report to the Registrar General within a month after.

**Why this matters for the pitch:** this is a concrete, small-but-real (₹35,064) example of the EC-to-recharge/lake-rejuvenation pipeline that directly supports the "money follows enforcement into recharge infrastructure" narrative — ties Section 3 directly to Section 5 (City of Lakes).
