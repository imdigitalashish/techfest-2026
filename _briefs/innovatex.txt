                                 InnovateX
InnovateX: The Future Habitats Challenge is a premier national innovation
competition calling on India’s youngest minds and future leaders to solve critical
urban and environmental challenges for a sustainable tomorrow. Hosted by
Techfest, IIT Bombay, Asia's Largest Science & Technology Festival, this challenge
is a platform for building practical, high-impact solutions from the ground up.

We are seeking young problem-solvers, creators, designers, and systems thinkers
who want to move beyond textbook concepts and create tangible prototypes that
can transform our cities, starting from their own neighborhoods and scaling up to
the national stage.



Competition Themes

Theme 1 : Underground Water Management

Domain Overview

IoT-based Underground Water Management focuses on using connected sensors,
monitoring systems, and data analytics to understand and preserve groundwater
resources. Cities face severe challenges in groundwater depletion, contamination,
illegal extraction, pipeline leakages, and unpredictable recharge rates. IoT
technologies can provide real-time insights into water levels, flow behaviour, and
quality parameters, enabling smarter and more sustainable water management.
Effective solutions in this domain contribute directly to sustainability and resource
security.


Challenge Question

How can IoT technologies be designed and deployed to effectively monitor,
protect, and manage underground water resources in an urban city? Propose an
innovative IoT-based solution that addresses a specific groundwater-related
challenge such as water level monitoring, contamination detection, illegal
extraction prevention, pipeline leakage detection, or aquifer recharge analysis.
Your solution should detail the problem, proposed technology, impact, and
feasibility within a framework.


Objectives and Deliverables

• A Specific Underground Water Problem: Participants should identify a clear
challenge related to groundwater levels, quality, usage, or distribution.

• Propose an IoT-Based Solution: Present a comprehensive solution using IoT
sensors, communication systems, and analytics tools, highlighting innovation,
scalability, and practicality.

• Demonstrate Technical Feasibility: Explain how the IoT system would
function, including sensor operation, data collection, network requirements, and
real-time monitoring features.

• Identify Operational Challenges: Address issues such as underground
connectivity, sensor durability, power consumption, maintenance, and data
reliability.

• Economic Analysis: Evaluate the cost-effectiveness of the proposed IoT
solution and suggest a sustainable model for large-scale adoption in cities.

• Environmental and Social Impact: Assess how the solution supports
groundwater conservation, reduces wastage, improves water quality, and benefits
communities.

Theme 2: Rising AQI Levels in Modern Urban Cities

Domain Overview

Air pollution has become one of the most critical environmental challenges in
rapidly urbanizing cities. Increasing vehicular emissions, industrial activity,
construction dust, and waste burning contribute to escalating Air Quality Index
(AQI) levels. Prolonged exposure to poor air quality leads to respiratory illnesses,
reduced visibility, and long-term environ- mental degradation. As cities continue
to grow, smart-city technologies must play a vital role in monitoring, predicting,
and reducing AQI to ensure healthier and more sustainable urban living.
Challenge Question

How can technologies be leveraged to monitor, predict, and reduce rising AQI
levels in modern urban environments? Propose an innovative solution using IoT
sensors, AI-driven air quality forecasting, adaptive traffic control, drone-based
monitoring, or urban planning strategies. Your solution should address a specific
pollution source and outline its benefits, feasibility, and implementation strategy

Objectives and Deliverables

• Address a specific problem: Participants should identify a primary contributor
to rising AQI-vehicular emissions, industrial zones, construction dust, waste
burning, etc.

• Propose an Innovative Solution: Develop a feasible, sustainable, and
scalable solution using smart technologies to reduce or control air pollution.

• Demonstrate Practical Feasibility: Explain how the proposed system
operates and justify its effectiveness in real-world scenarios.

• Implementation Plan: Provide a deployment strategy, including sensor
placement, data collection, analytics, and integration with existing city systems.

• Identify Potential Challenges: Discuss obstacles such as sensor calibration,
weather effects, maintenance, or system reliability.

• Analyze Economic Viability: Present a cost-effective plan for large-scale
adoption and long-term operation in urban regions.

• Evaluate Environmental Impact: Quantify how the solution contributes to
pollution reduction, public health improvement, and sustainability.



Theme 3: Rising plastic pollution in Coastal Cities

Domain Overview

Coastal plastic pollution has become one of the most critical environmental
challenges in rapidly urbanizing megacities. In a coastal hub like Mumbai,
increasing single-use plastic consumption, unsegregated municipal waste, open
dumping, and stormwater runoffs contribute to escalating marine debris. Survey
data estimates that approximately 5 million kilograms (5,000 metric tonnes)
of plastic waste flow annually from Mumbai's waterways, creeks, and
stormwater drains (nullahs) into the Arabian Sea. Prolonged accumulation of this
waste chokes critical mangrove ecosystems, severely disrupts marine life, and
worsens urban flooding during monsoons. As coastal cities continue to grow,
smart-city technologies, automated interception systems, and circular-economy
models must play a vital role in monitoring, capturing, and upcycling plastic waste
to ensure cleaner and more sustainable urban-marine living.

Challenge Question
How can technologies be leveraged to monitor, intercept, and reduce rising plastic
waste leakage into Mumbai's coastal and urban drainage networks? Propose an
innovative solution using IoT waste-level sensors, automated river/creek
skimmers,    AI-driven   sorting    mechanisms,      barrier-net   systems,    or
community-integrated recycling strategies. Your solution should address a specific
plastic waste source and outline its benefits, feasibility, and implementation
strategy.

Objectives and Deliverables
   ●​ Address a Specific Problem: Participants should identify a primary
      contributor to Mumbai's marine plastic pollution such as single-use plastics
      entering stormwater drains, creek-mouth accumulation, unsegregated
      household garbage, or beach litter.
   ●​ Propose an Innovative Solution: Develop a feasible, sustainable, and
      scalable hardware or software solution using smart technologies to
      intercept, clear, or upcycle waste effectively.
   ●​ Demonstrate Practical Feasibility: Explain how the proposed system
      operates in real-world coastal or urban environments and justify its
      mechanical or digital effectiveness.
   ●​ Implementation Plan: Provide a deployment strategy, including
      installation sites (e.g., specific creeks, drainage outlets like Malad or
      Trombay, or high-footfall coastal zones), data collection, analytics, and
      integration with existing municipal systems.
   ●​ Identify Potential Challenges: Discuss obstacles such as marine
      corrosion, tidal fluctuations, regular maintenance, monsoon overflow, or
      system reliability.
   ●​ Analyze Economic Viability: Present a cost-effective plan for large-scale
      adoption, maintenance, and long-term operation within urban regions.
   ●​ Evaluate Environmental Impact: Quantify how the solution contributes
      to plastic reduction, marine life preservation, urban flood prevention, and
      overall sustainability.

Participants are allowed to choose any one of the Theme from the above themes

Eligibility & Team Rules

   ● Team Size: A maximum of 4 members per team.

Competition Format

Round 1: Abstract Submission & Presentation

Round 1 requires teams to pitch their concept physically at their regional zonal
center, showcasing their proposed architecture, design flow, and feasibility plan.

Your submission should be clear, concise, and original, containing:
   ●​ Team Information – Team name, Team ID,Theme number and contact
      details for all members.
   ●​ Problem Statement – A precise description of the specific problem you are
      addressing from one of the 3 competition themes.
   ●​ Background & Context – Briefly mention existing solutions (if any) and why
      they fall short.
   ●​ Proposed Solution – A short explanation of your original and innovative
      idea, including the technology you plan to use and how it works.
   ●​ Impact & Scalability – A brief summary of the potential social and economic
      impact and how it can be scaled to benefit a large population.

Note : Each team will be getting 10 Mins of time to present their solution.

The Top five people from each regional zonal center will qualify for Round 2
All the students who qualified for round 2 , should start working on their
prototype.

Note :You do not need to make any submissions for Round 1; all submissions are
evaluated at the zonal venues.

Round 2: Final Report & Prototype Submission and Final Presentations
Submit a detailed report and working prototype including:
   ●​ Detailed Solution Description – How it works, its design, and technology
      used.
   ●​ Prototype Demonstration – Evidence of functionality and usability for target
      users
   ●​ Cost & Market Viability – Estimated cost, affordability, and potential market
      fit.
   ●​ Implementation Plan – Steps for deployment and adaptability to different
      conditions.
   ●​ Scalability Plan – How it can be expanded for wider impact across India.
   ●​ Impact Assessment – Clear estimation of social and economic benefits.


The final report must be submitted by December 1, 2026 on the website.

Evaluation, Prizes, and Recognition:

Evaluation Criteria
  ●​ Creativity and Novelty: How original and innovative is the proposed idea?
     How effectively does it introduce new smart technologies, IoT architectures,
     or mechanical approaches compared to existing solutions?
  ●​ Functionality and Usability: How well does the working prototype or
     simulation perform? Is the system reliable, accurate, and robust when
     operating under real-world environmental pressures (such as subterranean
     connectivity, marine corrosion, tidal shifts, or high-dust atmospheres)?
  ●​ Cost and Market Value: Is the solution economically viable and
     affordable? Does it present a sustainable financial model for long-term
     municipal, industrial, or community adoption?
  ●​ Technical Implementation & Feasibility: Is the solution technically
     feasible, repeatable, and resilient? How well does it account for operational
     challenges like power consumption, sensor calibration, maintenance, and
     severe weather conditions?
  ●​ Regional Scalability: Can the solution be easily scaled and adapted to
     diverse geographical, infrastructural, and regulatory landscapes across
     different cities and coastal hubs in India?
  ●​ Environmental & Socioeconomic Impact: What is the quantifiable
     impact on resource preservation, pollution mitigation (such as water
     conservation, AQI improvement, or marine plastic diversion), and public
     health or community well-being?
  ●​ Design & Ergonomics: Is the system architecture, hardware layout, or
     software interface well-thought-out, user-centric, and optimized for
     operators, municipal authorities, or local communities?
  ●​ Problem-Centric Approach (Bonus Points): Teams that demonstrate a
     deep, nuanced understanding of their chosen theme (Underground Water
     Management, Rising AQI, or Coastal Plastic Pollution in regions like
     Mumbai) will receive extra credit. This includes providing a detailed analysis
     of specific local pain points and showing how their solution directly targets
     those unique challenges rather than relying on a generic product.
