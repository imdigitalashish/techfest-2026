                  AI for Technology Readiness
Objective:

To develop functional AI-powered, AI-assisted, or hybrid assessment tools capable
of evaluating Technology Readiness Levels (TRL 1–9) from unstructured technical
documentation, streamlining technology transfer workflows, and providing
transparent, auditable evidence for innovation ecosystems across developing
nations.

Challenge Question:

How can an AI pipeline accurately parse raw technology documentation (e.g.,
patents, research papers, R&D reports), predict its correct Technology Readiness
Level (TRL 1–9), synthesize supporting evidence, and output an auditable,
actionable gap analysis to guide technology progression?

Note on Evaluation & Instructions: The detailed problem statement instructions,
specific dataset guidelines, assessment criteria checklist, and development sample
cases will be officially released to all registered teams on October 10, 2026,
immediately after registrations close.

Scope & Required Deliverables
The complete dataset guidelines, assessment criteria, sample development cases,
and detailed track specifications will be officially released to all registered teams on
October 10, 2026.
Format of Competition:
Round 1: AI Hackathon & Hidden Evaluation (Online):

Structure: Teams develop their AI model using approved public data sources. Upon
submission, models undergo a strict code/model freeze. Organizers execute frozen
models on an undisclosed private evaluation set.
Registered teams are allowed a maximum of 3 code submission attempts before
the Stage 1 deadline.

For the first 2 submissions, organizers will run automated testing on the code and
provide diagnostic feedback, including operational execution status (whether the
code runs successfully or fails) and benchmark performance scores.

The 3rd submission serves as the final, immutable entry for official Stage 1
ranking and code freeze

Evaluation Criteria (100% Performance-Based):

   ●​ Model Ordinal Accuracy & Precision on Hidden Evaluation Set (Primary Score)
   ●​ Strict pass/fail compliance on output schema, execution speed, and
      dependency checks

Round 2: System Hackathon & Grand Finale (IIT Bombay Campus)

Structure: National finalists integrate their Stage 1 frozen model into a working
end-to-end system. On Day 2, teams process undisclosed live-challenge technology
files, demonstrate system usability, and pitch to an expert panel.

Team Specification and Eligibility
   ●​ Age Limit: All participants must be 35 years of age or younger on
      December 17, 2026.
   ●​ Team Size: Teams of 1 to 3 members.
   ●​ Eligibility: Open to undergraduate/postgraduate students, researchers,
      independent developers, and industry professionals.
   ●​ Single Registration Rule: A participant can only be part of one team.
      Duplicate registrations lead to immediate disqualification.

General Rules of the Competition
  1.​ All teams from India must register on techfest.org to receive an official Team
      ID, which is mandatory for tracking and all submission pathways.
  2.​ Submissions are frozen at the Stage 1 deadline; no model retraining,
      fine-tuning, or replacement is permitted for Stage 2.
  3.​ Minor integration-related bug fixes during Stage 2 require explicit written
      authorization from the Technical Evaluation Lead.
  4.​ Submissions must represent the team's original work. Pre-existing code and
      model weights remain the property of the participants.
  5.​ Finalists must release their Stage 2 deployment package under the Apache
      License 2.0 for non-profit developmental use.
  6.​ Use of undisclosed private API keys, hardcoded output schemas, or
      plagiarism will result in immediate disqualification.
  7.​ The use of unauthorized external AI tools during live rounds is strictly
      prohibited unless explicitly allowed by the judges.
  8.​ Instructions for specific rounds will be communicated on time to registered
      email IDs, and official announcements will be posted on techfest.org.
  9.​ All contest queries must be submitted through the official portal's Doubt
      section.
  10.​Decisions made by the judges, Techfest Organizers, and the APCTT
      Evaluation Panel are final and binding on all participants.
