                           Techfest 2026-27

                              Namma Space
Objective:

From time to time there have been shifts in the trends of the world. Earlier, the idea
was that there is a possibility people moving to physical spaces will become
obsolete, but it has not happened yet, which shows that despite everything being
available online, there is an intrinsically better feel when people see physical
spaces. But as the online world is growing, at the scale at which businesses want to
grow, being digital is a vital part of it. Can we make something which is the best of
both worlds?

Namma Space challenges participants to break this barrier. Your mission is to build
an end-to-end intelligent spatial engine that converts standard captures of real
indoor environments into photorealistic, interactive 3D digital twins. The vision is to
completely replace static, outdated 2D blueprints with a scalable, automated
pipeline.

Imagine stepping into a sprawling IKEA or a massive corporate event space and not
knowing where to go. Instead of deciphering a complex floor plan, users simply
click a link and are instantly immersed in a 3D web environment. The platform must
enable remote visitors to conduct seamless virtual walkthroughs, tag and search
spatial Points of Interest (POIs), and calculate non-colliding walkable paths through
an accessible web interface. Ultimately, this engine should guide users directly to
their destination with the same intuitive ease that Google Maps provides for the
outdoors, successfully routing users around physical obstacles like walls and
furniture.

Challenge Question:

How would you design and build a complete software pipeline that turns ordinary
smartphone photos or videos of an indoor space into an interactive 3D web
application: one that lets remote users freely explore the space in first person,
search for a specific room or item and jump straight to it, and see the best walking
path to reach it around real physical obstacles?
Build an end-to-end system that reconstructs an interactive 3D model of a real
indoor space from data users can realistically provide (images, video, floor plans,
sensor data), and adds navigation and search on top of it.

The end goal is live, in-person navigation: think Google Maps walking directions,
but indoors, guided through the phone camera.

A user with no technical expertise should be able to:

   ●​ Capture a real indoor space (an office floor, a retail store, a mall section)
      using only a smartphone.
   ●​ Reconstruct it into a photorealistic 3D model with no manual 3D modelling.
   ●​ Explore the space remotely in a web browser, walking through it freely like a
      video-game walkthrough.
   ●​ Navigate the space on-site through their phone: pick a destination like
      "Meeting Room 4" or "3-seater sofas" and get guided to it through the live
      camera view, with directions updating as they walk.

Scope & Required Deliverables
Participants must build a solution encompassing the following sequential steps.
Since depth analysis and stitching can be highly complex for large corporate areas,
teams are free to utilize any technology currently available in the market.

   ●​ SOP Construction & 3D Modeling: Participants must define their own
      Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) for data collection. This SOP dictates
      how the space is captured (e.g., specifying camera angles, corner shots,floor
      plan or utilizing sensors). The captured data - whether it involves manually
      stitching different rooms or automatically processing a single large video file
      - must be used to construct a reliable 3D model.
   ●​ Virtual Desktop Walkthrough: The platform must allow users to freely
      roam and look around the generated 3D space through a zero-install desktop
      or web-view interface. Users should experience fluid, first-person movement
      using standard controls like WASD keys and mouse inputs. This must
      function smoothly even without active navigation running.
   ●​ Point of Interest (POI) Labeling: The 3D space must support POI tagging
      (e.g., "Reception", "Meeting Room A"). Users should be able to click on
      specific rooms or zones within the model to reveal their designated names.
   ●​ Desktop Navigation: Once a POI is selected, the UI must provide active
      path-planning and navigation, visually guiding the user to the destination
      similar to movement in a video game. This desktop navigation is mandatory
      and must compute optimal walking paths that stay on the floor and avoid
      physical obstacles.
   ●​ AR Navigation (Bonus): As an advanced bonus step, teams can implement
      Augmented Reality (AR) navigation for mobile devices.

Incentive: Participants who complete the full pipeline end to end, including AR
navigation will earn the opportunity to be considered for a full time job opportunity
at Juspay.

Format of Competition:

Round 1: 3D Reconstruction & Roaming (Online)
  ●​ Focus: SOP construction, 3D model building, and free-roaming
  ●​ Format: Online submission via Techfest portal.
  ●​ Dataset: Participants test and map their own home (or a similarly accessible
     indoor space) based on their defined Standard Operating Procedure (SOP).
  ●​ Deliverables: Submit a Round 1 Google Drive folder link ("Anyone with the
     link can view") containing:
         ○​ Technical Abstract PDF (max 5 pages): System architecture,
             pipeline design, SOP construction details, and technical approach.
         ○​ Proof of Concept & Web Viewer Link: A document containing a
             working link to the web-viewer allowing free-roaming.
         ○​ Demonstration Video: A 3 to 5-minute video detailing the project
             scope, capture process, and showing the 3D web walkthrough.
         ○​ Code Repository: A link/folder containing setup instructions to
             reconstruct the model, along with sample data placed inside a test
             directory.
  ●​ Evaluation Criteria: SOP & Pipeline Soundness, Visual Quality & Model
     Fidelity, Web Viewer Performance, and Documentation & Video Clarity

Round 2: POIs & Navigation (Online)
  ●​ Focus: POI labeling and desktop pathfinding UI.
  ●​ Format: Online submission via Techfest portal.
  ●​ Deliverables: Submit an updated Round 2 Google Drive folder ("Anyone
     with the link can view") containing:​

         ○​ Updated Code Repository Link: A document pointing to the
            repository branch containing POI and navigation implementations.
         ○​ Live Web App Link: A working URL demonstrating the ability to click
            on POIs, execute spatial searches, and follow generated walkable floor
            paths.
         ○​ Demonstration Video: A 3 to 5-minute video showing search
            execution, POI interaction, and obstacle-aware path visualization.​
  ●​ Evaluation Criteria: Spatial Indexing & POI Tagging, Pathfinding Accuracy,
     Search UI & Transition, and Code Cleanliness & Repository Setup.

Round 3: On-Site Capture & Live Finale (On Campus at IIT Bombay)

  ●​ Focus: Mapping a completely new location.
  ●​ Format: On-site data collection, execution, and live presentation at the IIT
     Bombay campus.​

        ○​ Day 1 (On-Site Data Capture & Processing): Qualifying teams will
           be assigned a specific physical venue on campus (e.g., inside the
           Lecture Hall Complex - LHC). Teams will collect data on-site using their
           required hardware/sensors according to their SOP, execute their
           pipeline, reconstruct the 3D digital twin, annotate POIs, and deploy
           their full interactive platform within the given timeframe.
        ○​ Day 2 (Live Presentation Phase): Live demonstration, pitch, and
           technical Q&A with expert judges on campus.​

  ●​ Deliverables to Present:
       ○​ 10-Minute Live Pitch & Platform Demo: Demonstration of the
           custom venue capture, reconstructed 3D web platform, POI search,
           and walking path navigation live in front of the panel.
       ○​ 5-Minute Technical Q&A: Cross-examination by judges regarding
           capture SOP efficiency, processing turnaround time, pathfinding
           accuracy, and pipeline scalability.​

  ●​ Evaluation Criteria:
       ○​ SOP Execution & Field Capture (20%): Efficiency and practicality
          of the team's data collection method on-site.
       ○​ Reconstruction & Visual Quality (20%): Fidelity, structural
          integrity, and visual clarity of the reconstructed venue.
       ○​ Web Viewer & UX (20%): Responsiveness, fluid exploration, and
          movement performance inside the browser.
       ○​ Spatial Search & Path Calculation (20%): Accuracy of POI
          tagging, instant search transitions, and non-colliding pathing around
          physical obstacles.
       ○​ Final Pitch & Technical Q&A (20%): Clarity of live demonstration
          and technical depth during judge Q&A.
       ○​ On-Site AR Navigation (Bonus/10%): Accuracy of real-time
          camera tracking and live directional arrow overlays on-site.
