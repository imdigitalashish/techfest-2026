# Pre-registration — interval coverage of moderated block estimates (spike 2) — SYNTHETIC data

- **Registered:** 2026-10-04 15:44 IST (this text is committed in the same git commit as the frozen code it describes; the commit time is the evidence).
- **Registered by:** project lead (india71-t2-learning). Requested by the Supervisor (definition of done item 1, 4 Oct 15:24).
- **Status of held-out data:** held-out seeds **101–130 have not been run** with any version of this method before this commit. Development used seeds **1–30** only.

## Question
Do the published 90 % intervals for each block's share of Grade-3 children meeting the reading goal cover the true share at least 85 % of the time on held-out blocks?

## Frozen method (files in this commit)
- Estimator: `components/trust_audit/assurance.py` — risk strata (K = 4 quantile groups of the public risk score) → re-test misclassification correction from a 20 % double-assessed subsample → stratum reporting-bias model (bias_model = "mean": children-weighted Horvitz–Thompson stratum means, unclipped corrected re-test shares) → Fay–Herriot model on the bias (direct = block's HT-weighted re-test residual; synthetic = stratum model; between-block variance by an adjusted likelihood that is strictly positive) → precision-weighted benchmarking to an independent same-construct survey anchor.
- Intervals: parametric bootstrap of the whole pipeline (R = 200; pseudo-truths with block effects and school deviations; re-drawn audit selection, re-tests, double checks and anchor) → basic bootstrap 90 % interval. Published method = `fh` (benchmarked).
- Design: risk-PPS audit selection (`select_audit`, design "risk_pps") of 10 % of schools; 12 children re-tested per school; simulator `eval/sim/world.py` with `eval/sim/calibration.py` (ASER 2024 govt Std III: 23.4 % reading, 27.6 % subtraction); 600 schools, 6 blocks per district, 30 % inflating schools; assessor error 3 % (symmetric, independent).
- Seeds per world: audit selection seed+17, re-test seed+31, bootstrap seed+53.

## Primary test (pass/fail)
- **Command:** `python3 eval/coverage_test.py --seeds 101-130 --out eval/results/coverage_heldout.json`
- **Statistic:** share of the 180 held-out blocks (30 worlds × 6 blocks) whose true share lies inside the method-`fh` 90 % interval.
- **PASS iff coverage ≥ 0.85.** Also required (secondary gate): moderated block MAE < raw teacher-reported block MAE on the same blocks.
- Reported regardless: Wilson 95 % interval for coverage, mean half-width, mean error, coverage of the unbenchmarked `direct` interval.

## Secondary analyses (descriptive, same held-out seeds, run once)
1. Numeracy: `--subject numeracy --out eval/results/coverage_heldout_numeracy.json`
2. Budgets: `--budget 0.05 --out eval/results/coverage_heldout_b05.json` and `--budget 0.20 --out eval/results/coverage_heldout_b20.json`
3. Stress — need-correlated inflation (lower-performing schools more likely to inflate): `--need-corr 1.0 --out eval/results/coverage_heldout_needcorr.json`
4. **Allocation (decision rule fixed now):** in every run, the top 10 % of schools are targeted using (a) raw reports, (b) moderated school estimates, (c) truth (oracle); metric = true number of below-goal children in targeted schools (effect-free; no assumed camp effect). **"Allocation gain shown" iff the paired 95 % CI of (moderated − raw) over the 30 worlds is entirely above 0.** Reported for the default and the need-correlated scenario whatever the outcome.

## Development record (seeds 1–30 only)
- Legacy method (school SEs combined in quadrature): coverage 0.27 (5 seeds, `eval/results/summary.md` of 4 Oct 09:54).
- Found and fixed: (i) clipping of misclassification-corrected re-test shares at 0 biased low-performing strata upward; (ii) school-weighted instead of children-weighted bias; (iii) block-level deviations of reporting bias (a district-wide model over-corrects honest high-performing blocks) → Fay–Herriot on the bias.
- Final method on dev seeds 1–30 (180 blocks): coverage 0.928 (Wilson 95 % 0.880–0.957); MAE 2.30 pp vs raw 4.08 pp; mean half-width 5.97 pp. Allocation on dev: moderated − raw = −3.6 below-goal children (95 % CI −10.5 to +3.3) → not shown.

## Commitments
- The held-out runs are executed once, after this commit, with the commands above; results are committed as produced.
- No change to the method, seeds, thresholds or metrics after seeing held-out results. If the test fails, the proposal and the selection memo say so.

## Known simulator assumptions (all results are SYNTHETIC)
Classroom-tool responses are simulated as un-manipulated (they feed the risk score); inflation is independent across schools except in the stress scenario; assessor errors are symmetric and independent between the two assessors; the anchor measures the same construct in the same population and year. Real-world validity requires a pilot.
