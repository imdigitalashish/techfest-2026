3 · The alarm dial: how sensitive should "detect → retrain" be? post-hoc demo sweep, not pre-registered

The textbook recipe: run a rolling two-sample Kolmogorov–Smirnov test of the last 10 minutes against the 2023 training data. When it rejects at level α, retrain on the post-alarm data. Turn the dial: the replay above and the 22-day held-out panel below are recomputed for that α (each stop was precomputed by the committed code).
