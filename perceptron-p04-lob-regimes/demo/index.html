Order-book forecasting under regime shifts: the alarm that cries wolf

This is a forecaster for the 10-second direction of the Binance USD-M BTCUSDT mid-price, on 5-level books. It was trained on 12 days of 2023, tuned on 2024, and tested on 22 held-out days (2025-01 … 2026-10).
