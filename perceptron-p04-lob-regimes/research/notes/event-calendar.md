# Event Calendar: First-of-Month US Macro Releases & Crypto Shocks (2023-01-01 to 2025-12-01)

Research context: Tardis.dev binance-futures BTCUSDT CSVs are free only on the first day of each
month. This file catalogs known-in-advance US macro release timestamps on those dates (FOMC,
NFP/Employment Situation, ISM Manufacturing PMI) plus any notable unscheduled crypto shocks that
happened to land on a first-of-month date, for use as exogenous changepoint labels.

Accessed: 2026-10-04

## Timezone conversion rule used
- EDT = UTC-4: 2023-03-12 to 2023-11-05; 2024-03-10 to 2024-11-03; 2025-03-09 to 2025-11-02
- EST = UTC-5: all other dates
- FOMC statements released 14:00 ET (2:00 PM)
- NFP / Employment Situation released 08:30 ET
- ISM Manufacturing PMI released 10:00 ET

verified=yes: confirmed against an official primary source (federalreserve.gov, bls.gov, ismworld.org)
