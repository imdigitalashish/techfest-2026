- The selected dev JSON records KS at **18:09:32** and harm at **18:17:43** on 2024-05-01, so the rounded alarm times are real. It also records alarms earlier that day. These are event-relative timestamps, not independently validated detection delays.
- Exercised the **actual `HarmCUSUM` class**, extracted in memory with Python's AST, without importing the pipeline or writing caches. For 20,000 positive synthetic losses with identical marginal values and mean excess over `ln 2` of **-0.0366**, repeating each draw ten times produced **31 alarms**, versus **0** after shuffling the same values, using threshold 20, minimum 60 labels, cooldown 60. Seed 8; increments were sampled from `[-0.38, +0.30]`, then added to `ln 2`. This is a dependence-sensitivity demonstration, **not** an estimate of BTC false alarms or a realistic market simulation.
- **No direct feature/label look-ahead found in the inspected paths:** backward receive-time as-of joins use `side="right" - 1`; feature windows trail; EWMA uses pre-update statistics; `j = t - H` makes labels available after prediction; regime edges are fitted on training inputs; walk-forward contexts use only days `x < d`, with stride 10. Precomputing a causal transform is not itself leakage. The prior holdout exposure in A1 is a different, research-design problem.
- Did **not** rerun the heavy evaluation, reconstruct raw books, validate file hashes or clock synchronization, or establish live-feed/execution correctness. Read the existing tests but did not rerun their suite. Did not download or independently reopen the cited papers/fee pages; comments about UQ-LOB, Jeon and Podkopaev-Ramdas are bounded by the repository's quotations and descriptions, not a full-paper novelty audit.

**Statistical choices that are reasonable:** Log-loss is an appropriate primary proper score for a probabilistic direction task, conditional on stating the non-tie estimand. Day-cluster resampling is much more defensible than pretending overlapping ten-second labels are independent. A 97.5% two-sided CI for each of exactly two fixed comparisons provides a Bonferroni 95% simultaneous two-sided family; requiring the upper endpoint below zero is actually conservative for directional improvement claims, with a nominal combined upper-tail error of at most 2.5%. That argument does not transfer automatically to the added secondary comparisons or overcome the sampling limitations below.
