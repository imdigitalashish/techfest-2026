## TL;DR
1. India's own charger counts **disagree across sources in the same window**: WRI India 34,629 stations/5.4M EVs = 1:156 (Nov'24) [WRI] vs PIB/CEA 25,202 (Oct'24) vs Down To Earth/ICCT ~12,000, 1:191 ratio (Oct'24) [DTE] vs IEEFA 5,151(2022)→52,718(Jul'26) [IEEFA2]. No single "ground truth" exists — argues for a transparent integration+uncertainty layer, not picking one feed.
2. **Zero prior art anywhere** for capture-recapture/multiple-systems-estimation applied to EV charger completeness — confirmed via exhaustive negative search (§g). Genuine novel-contribution opportunity.
3. **No EV-charging-specific entity-resolution method exists** (Splink/dedupe have zero POI examples in their own docs) — but Open Charge Map's real production dedup code is readable and explicitly admits PlugShare-matching is unsolved (§f) — best concrete baseline to beat.
4. 2024–26 siting literature has real validated-on-real-data bi-level/queueing/GNN/LLM papers (Montreal, Changchun, Hefei, Dallas, Hanoi, Chengdu, Scotland) but **zero apply classic FRLM to an Indian highway corridor** despite PM E-DRIVE's own 25km-spacing rule inviting exactly that (§b).
5. 2SFCA accessibility metrics are validated for EV charging in China (3 papers) but **never for India**; no India drive-time-isochrone EV study exists either (§c).
6. EU AFIR gives exact legal kW/BEV targets (1.3 kW/BEV, 0.8 kW/PHEV, Art.3) — directly adaptable as an India benchmark since **no India equivalent exists**; also **no formal numeric "charging desert" threshold exists anywhere in the literature** (§d).
7. Reliability is bad and unintegrated into "available infra" counts everywhere checked: Delhi field survey 83.7% non-functional [IEEFA1]; Bay Area peer-reviewed 73.3% functional of 655 EVSEs vs EVSPs' claimed 95–98% [Rempel]; US J.D. Power: 14% of all 2025 charging visits failed [JDP].
8. 2026 SOTA forecasting (Gaussian-process neighbourhood disaggregation, beats logistic-growth baseline) has never been applied to India/VAHAN [NatComms] — directly transplantable, high-value gap.
9. Maintained OSS exists for two hard sub-problems never applied to EV chargers: `pysal/spopt` (381★, siting) and `splink` (2,452★, entity resolution).
10. Both India-specific "EV demand prediction" GitHub repos found have **0 stars** — no serious open India tool exists yet.

## (a) Demand estimation (VAHAN/mobility/POI) — India focus
- **CSTEP, "Bengaluru 2030: EV Charging Demand & Infrastructure"** (Aparna K P, Aniket Wakhare, CSTEP-RR-2024-07) [CSTEP, verified]: survey-driven model → **2.3M EVs by 2030 (>85% e-2W), >3Mt CO₂/yr avoided**; estimates required "charger guns," grid impact, siting, 2030 tariffs. Maturity: policy report, not peer-reviewed. *Beat-it angle*: replace city survey with VAHAN time series + POI proxies to generalize nationally.
- **WRI India** [WRI, verified]: 34,629 public stations / 5.4M EVs = **1:156** (Nov 2024); runs spatial-analysis siting support for Mumbai/Surat/Lucknow; built an open **FACtTool** (financial viability Excel tool) — the one reusable open India artifact found.
- **ICCT India BET-truck working paper** (Aug 2025) [ICCT, landing page verified]: state-level depot+highway charging-needs methodology for e-trucks using road-freight data, 2030/2050 horizons. Headline numbers (9GW/130k trucks/70%-in-5-states) are **UNVERIFIED** — PDF unreadable (binary) in this session.
- **IIT Bombay Grid Integration Lab** (Rather group) [IITB-GIL, verified]: active, substantial IEEE-published EV-grid body of work; a specific VAHAN-based demand report (Rather/Nath/Lekshmi/Banerjee) exists per secondary citation but its PDF/page were **UNVERIFIED** (broken link / unparseable).
- **GRAAM × Mercedes-Benz R&D India** [GRAAM, verified]: VAHAN/Parivahan data, 1,464 RTOs/36 states/UTs, extracted 15 Jun 2026. Exact national EV-adoption-rate series: **2.46%(FY21-22)→5.26%→6.80%→7.49%→8.25%(FY25-26)**; 126.23M total registrations, 8.02M EVs (6.36% aggregate); state leaders by rate **Tripura 12.40%, Delhi 11.18%**. Best-verified India calibration/backtest series found.
- **Spatial disaggregation SOTA**: Flower, Li & Padget, *Nature Communications* 2026, GP-based neighbourhood disaggregation [NatComms, verified abstract]: beats logistic-growth/scaled-scenario baselines, validated on 1,294 England/Wales neighbourhoods, significant gains at 3yr+ horizons — not yet applied to VAHAN (gap).
- NREL EVI-Pro and ICCT's global VPC-ratio methodology: **UNVERIFIED** (DNS failure / fetch timeout respectively) — mechanism descriptions only from search synthesis, do not quote numbers.
- India GitHub repos `saeeshk/EV-Charging-Station-Demand-Prediction-in-Indian-Cities` and `ipooja21/EV-Charging-Station-Predictor` [GH-India, verified via API]: **0 stars each** — confirms no serious open India tool exists.

## (b) Siting / location-allocation
Classics (all verified via Crossref API) [Classics-b]: **MCLP** (Church&ReVelle 1974), **p-median** (Hakimi 1964), **set-covering** (Toregas et al. 1971), **flow-capturing** (Hodgson 1990), **FRLM** (Kuby&Lim 2005, DOI 10.1016/j.seps.2004.03.001).
- **FRLM/corridor 2024-26**: Sugishita/Carvalho/Atallah cyclic-FRLP, arXiv:2409.04554 [arXiv-b], **30% fewer facilities** than original FRLP on a California benchmark. AL_HAMEEDAWI&Ozen, *Sustainability* 2025, FCLM+FRLM on **real Baghdad district** — VALIDATED ON REAL DATA. **No paper applies FRLM to an Indian highway corridor** — confirmed gap (4 distinct searches, all empty); PM E-DRIVE's "charger every 25km" rule is policy-only, not a peer-reviewed FRLM study.
- **Bi-level**: exact B&P&C algorithm, arXiv:2511.19884, **<1% optimality gap, 3–50× faster** than GA baselines (MA/Anaheim/Barcelona networks). Ma et al., *Algorithms* 2025, **Hefei City real network** — VALIDATED. Li et al., *Modelling* 2026, **Nei-Yi Expressway (real highway)** — VALIDATED.
- **Queueing-aware**: Nguyen/Sugishita/Carvalho/Dems, arXiv:2510.12961, M/M/s/K bilevel, **real Montreal case study** — VALIDATED. Wang et al., *WEVJ* 2025, **Changchun real data**: optimal = 15 stations/110 fast+40 slow chargers/11,544 kVA, **system cost −4.31%**.
- **Grid-constrained**: Kaushik/Wang/Jacob/Zhang, arXiv:2509.23699, **Dallas real charger network + 8,500-bus system** — hybrid/validated. Mukherjee, arXiv:2604.10997, CIGRE synthetic network only (note: an AI-search summary of this paper was wrong — verified abstract says redistribution *can degrade* service, opposite of the summary's "+74%" claim — always re-check primary text).
- **ML/RL/GNN/LLM 2024-26**: Nguyen/Le/Nguyen RL, arXiv:2511.01218, **Hanoi real data, 53.28% wait-time reduction**. Batic/Stankovic/Stankovic GNN, *Energy* 2025, **Glasgow/Edinburgh real data**, equity-aware siting. **Manzoor, Lall & Panigrahi (IIT Delhi-affiliated)**, GraphSAGE, IEEE SEFET 2025 [IITD-GNN]: real multi-domain dataset, **ROC-AUC 0.842, F1 0.824** — closest India-academic GNN-siting paper found. Zheng/Jiang/Wang LLM-assisted formulation, arXiv:2511.19055, **1.5M real Chengdu records, 30% cost reduction**. **EV-Planner**, *Frontiers in AI* 2026, code at `github.com/bharathanand0/EV-Planner` (2★): **real 10-US-states data, 52.3% distance reduction, 71.7% fewer overloaded stations** — most directly adaptable paper+code combo found.
- **Code**: `pysal/spopt` (381★, BSD-3, pushed 2026-10-02, actively maintained) — classic MCLP/p-median/LSCP, best production-grade library, not EV-specific. Most India-tagged repos are 0–30★ and unmaintained. GitHub searches for "FRLM"/"EV charging bilevel"/"charging station siting India" etc. **returned zero real repos**.

## (c) Spatial accessibility (2SFCA family)
Classics, healthcare-origin [2SFCA-classics, secondary-sourced except where noted]: 2SFCA (Radke&Mu 2000/Luo&Wang 2003, binary catchment); **E2SFCA** (Luo&Qi 2009, tiered decay); **Gaussian-2SFCA** (Dai 2010); **3SFCA** (Wan/Zou/Sternberg 2012, demand-competition step); **i2SFCA** (Wang 2018, inverted — directly verified via PMC7959250); a Huff-based variable-catchment hybrid close to "MH3SFCA" exists only for healthcare.
- **Applied to EV charging (2023-25, China only)** [2SFCA-EV, verified via Semantic Scholar abstracts]: Chen/Chen/Lu 2023 (Gaussian-2SFCA, China-wide: east 69.1% of station density, **west most inequitable**); Sheng et al. 2024 (G2SFCA+MGWR+RF, Guangzhou); Chen/Moore/Jia/Zhang/Cao 2025 (review of **91 studies**, flags geographic gap outside NA/EU/China explicitly).
- **Confirmed gaps**: no 2SFCA-for-EV-charging paper for India; **no India drive-time-isochrone EV-accessibility study exists at all**.

## (d) Gap / equity / "charging desert" benchmarks
- **EU AFIR** Reg. (EU) 2023/1804 Art.3 [AFIR, verified exact EUR-Lex text]: **1.3 kW/BEV + 0.8 kW/PHEV** cumulative power targets from 2024; TEN-T core pools **≥400kW by 2025 → ≥600kW by 2027** every 60km, incl. ≥1(then 2) points ≥150kW; comprehensive network phased to 2035. No India equivalent exists (gap).
- **EVs/charger ratios**: US DOE **10:1** planning benchmark [Paren, verified]. India: WRI **1:156** (Nov'24) vs Down To Earth citing ICCT Oct'24 **1:191**, "2 chargers/100,000 people" [DTE, verified] — these disagree, report both.
- **Gini/Lorenz**: Ranjgar/Niccolai/Leva 2025 + reproducing code `github.com/Babak-RJ-AZ/evcs-spatial-equity-atlas` [Gini-EV, verified]: OCM-API-driven, exact Gini **0.42(Amsterdam)–0.61(Naples)** population-based across 4 EU cities — directly reusable methodology+code for India.
- **"Charging desert"**: **no formal numeric threshold found anywhere** in DOE/NREL/academic literature [Paren, verified] — confirmed gap, do not invent one without flagging it as original.
- No India-specific formal spatial-equity (2SFCA/Gini) study found — gap.

## (e) Adoption forecasting
- **Bass (1969)** classic [Bass, verified via Crossref], *Management Sci.* 15(5):215-227 — innovator/imitator diffusion, ancestor of logistic-curve EV forecasts.
- **SOTA**: Flower/Li/Padget 2026 *Nat. Comms* [NatComms, verified] — GP framework beats logistic-growth/scaled-scenario, significant from 3yr+ horizons, 1,294 UK neighbourhoods. Not yet run on India.
- **RMI** [RMI, verified]: estimates India needs **>1.9M public chargers + 275,000 battery-swap stations by 2040** to hit 30% EV penetration by 2030 — top-down target, method undisclosed on page fetched.
- Hierarchical-Bayesian US state-pooling paper (SSRN): **UNVERIFIED**, fetch blocked (403); approach (Bayesian logistic diffusion + state-ceiling regression + partial pooling) described only via secondary synthesis, not confirmed.
- GRAAM's FY21-26 series (§a) is the best India ground-truth for backtesting any Bass/logistic/Bayesian/GP model — **no published rigorously-backtested India-specific forecast found** (gap/opportunity).

## (g) Capture-recapture / multiple-systems estimation (MSE)
- Classics (Lincoln-Petersen, Chapman, Fienberg 1972 log-linear MSE) — ecology→census-undercount→conflict-casualties→trafficking-prevalence; **not independently re-fetched**, background only.
- **Barrington-Leigh & Millard-Ball (2017)**, *PLoS ONE* [OSM-complete, verified]: global OSM road-completeness **~83%**, via regression+poststratification + sigmoid saturation curves. **Explicitly NOT capture-recapture** (no overlap/m₂ term at all).
- **Confirmed: capture-recapture/MSE is not an established technique anywhere in the OSM/VGI-completeness literature**, and **zero prior art exists for applying it to EV charger databases specifically** — exhaustive, differently-phrased searching, every result explicitly negative. **This is a genuine, confirmed white space** — directly stated per the brief's honesty requirement, not a forced match.

## (h) Charger reliability / uptime
- **J.D. Power 2025 EVX study** [JDP, verified via BusinessWire mirror]: 7,428 owners surveyed Jan–Jun 2025; DCFC satisfaction **654/1000 (−10pt YoY)**; **14% of all visits failed to charge** (down from 19% in 2024); **60%** of failures = charger out of service.
- **Rempel/Cullen/Bryan/Cezar (2024)**, *Human Factors* [Rempel, verified via OSTI]: Bay Area, **182 stations/655 EVSEs, 73.3% functional**; EVSPs self-report **95–98%** uptime — large, directly-documented discrepancy vs field measurement. (Note: an earlier 2022 press cycle cited 72.5%/181 stations — use the 2024 peer-reviewed 73.3% as authoritative.)
- **Asensio et al., NBER/Brookings 2025** [Asensio, verified, full PDF]: only **32.9%/34%** of US interstate DCFC stations report real-time status to PlugShare; gaps up to **1,308 miles**.
- **Yu/Zhu/Cushing (2025)**, *Nat. Comms* [YuZhu, verified]: US has 55,000 public chargers, only **37,000 (67%) fully accessible**; disadvantaged communities have **64% fewer** chargers/capita.
- **India — converging evidence, multiple independent sources**:
  - IEEFA Delhi field survey, Jun 2024 [IEEFA1, verified full PDF]: cluster-sampled **37 chargers, 31 non-functional = 83.7%** (explicitly caveated as "not all Delhi chargers, a representative sample").
  - IEEFA Sept 2026 [IEEFA2, verified via HTML]: stations grew **5,151(2022)→52,718(Jul 2026)**; explicitly recommends tying subsidies to verified uptime.
  - Down To Earth [DTE, verified]: **"50-60% functional"** (NITI Aayog-sourced, early 2024), **"nearly half non-functional"** (TATA.ev/Hindu-sourced).
  - Exicom 2026 industry whitepaper [Exicom, verified]: **"12,100 of 25,000 non-functional"** (Feb 2024), frames a **"scale-reliability gap."**
  - **Convergent finding**: every India source independently lands in the **50-85% functional range** — consistent directional signal despite no single authoritative continuously-updated dataset existing.

## Implications for our design (condensed)
1. **Entity resolution**: Splink (probabilistic core) seeded by OCM's own cheap deterministic cascade as a blocking pass; consider LLM-assisted resolution for the hard residual cases OCM's code admits it can't solve. *Novel: no EV-charging ER benchmark/dataset exists publicly — build one.*
2. **Completeness estimation**: Chapman/log-linear MSE over entity-resolved multi-source lists (OCM+PlugShare+CPO APIs+govt lists). *Novel: zero prior art anywhere — your single cleanest "transparency over proprietary data" win.*
3. **Reliability layer**: crowdsourced/API uptime polling + periodic field audits (IEEFA-style), reported at port-level; derate nameplate capacity into "operational capacity." *Novel: no continuously-updated India uptime dataset exists.*
4. **Demand/forecasting**: VAHAN (GRAAM-style cleaning) + GP neighbourhood-disaggregation (Flower et al. method) backtested against logistic/Bass baselines on the GRAAM FY21-26 series. *Novel: method never applied to India.*
5. **Siting**: `pysal/spopt` baseline (MCLP/p-median) + queueing-aware bi-level layer for corridors/urban cores; EV-Planner's fairness-constrained clustering as a template. *Novel: no FRLM ever applied to an Indian highway corridor.*
6. **Accessibility/equity index**: Gaussian-2SFCA (drive-time via India-OSM OSRM) + Gini/Lorenz (reuse Babak-RJ-AZ code) + AFIR-style kW/EV target (proposed, since none exists for India) + reliability-derated capacity, combined into one transparent composite "underserved area" index. *Novel: no source combines all of these, for any country.*
