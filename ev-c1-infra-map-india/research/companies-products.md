### 1.18 Delhi — "Switch Delhi" campaign / "One Delhi" app

- Background: "Switch Delhi" launched under the Delhi EV Policy (notified Aug 2020); at launch, tender floated for "**100 public charging stations**"; "6,000 EVs sold" since the policy. [ET Auto, 2020](https://auto.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/industry/kejriwal-launches-switch-delhi-campaign-says-6000-evs-sold-after-state-policy/80686783) — accessed 2026-10-04 — maturity: historical. Open database launched at `ev.delhi.gov.in/openev/` showing "**around 2,500 charging points**," target "**18,000 by 2025**" (Transport Minister Kailash Gahlot). [Hindustan Times, Aug 2022](https://www.hindustantimes.com/cities/delhi-news/delhi-govt-launches-database-of-all-e-vehicle-charging-points-battery-swapping-facilities-101663092638468.html) — accessed 2026-10-04 — maturity: production (at launch). "One Delhi" app relaunched **2 Nov 2022** adding "**more than 2,200 charging points**" (charger type + paid/free status), built by DDC Delhi + IIIT-Delhi. [The Hindu, 3 Nov 2022](https://www.thehindu.com/news/cities/Delhi/revamped-one-delhi-app-launched-in-city/article66086950.ece) — accessed 2026-10-04 — maturity: production (2022).
- **Direct technical verification performed 2026-10-04**: `ev.delhi.gov.in`'s TLS certificate (issued by GoDaddy) **expired 27 Aug 2025** — confirmed by direct certificate inspection — causing standard HTTPS clients to fail. Once reached (bypassing cert validation), the homepage shows no visible charging-locator map content in a simplified fetch; the `/openev/` open-data sub-path (2022-era public API) now returns **HTTP 502 "Sorry! We will be back soon."** — down/non-functional as of access date. "One Delhi" app is confirmed live on the Apple App Store (id1641749010) with an "EV filter" feature per its changelog, but current 2025/26 data-accuracy of the EV locator specifically could not be confirmed. [Direct cert/HTTP checks + Apple App Store listing] — accessed 2026-10-04.
- **Ground-check of physical chargers**: India Today TV found widespread non-functionality at central Delhi sites — Central Secretariat (6 stations, only 1 operational point), Talkatora Stadium (3 of 4 non-functional), Rajghat Bus Depot (all non-functional), Kashmere Gate/Ring Road (2 of 3 not working). [India Today, 2 Jul 2026](https://www.indiatoday.in/cities/delhi/story/delhi-ev-charging-stations-ground-check-finds-many-chargers-defunct-despite-new-policy-2939151-2026-07-02) — accessed 2026-10-04 — maturity: production infrastructure, widespread reported non-functionality.
- **Scale trend** (each figure as separately reported, not reconciled): ~2,500 points (Aug 2022, target 18,000 by 2025) → **8,849 charging points** as of 31 Dec 2025 (vs. estimated requirement of 36,150; plus 893 battery-swapping stations) → **"over 10,000"** by 16 Apr 2026 (BSES discoms: 6,500+ points across 3,400+ locations; TPDDL: 3,783 EV connections; Delhi #1 nationally in EV public-charging electricity consumption at 24.1% of India's total per CEA data). **The original "18,000 by 2025" target was missed**; the government's own revised 2026 goal (16,000) is still below the original promise. [AlCircle, Jan 2026](https://www.alcircle.com/news/delhi-government-eyeing-an-ev-ramp-up-with-7000-charging-stations-in-2026-116986); [Economic Times, 16 Apr 2026](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/industry/renewables/delhis-ev-charging-infra-registers-record-expansion-total-charging-points-over-10000/articleshow/130292510.cms) — accessed 2026-10-04 — maturity: production. July 2026: a **separate** new "Delhi EV Subsidy Portal" (`evsubsidy.delhi.gov.in`) launched for subsidy applications (not charger-location) under the "Delhi Electric Vehicle Policy 2026." [Daily Pioneer](https://www.dailypioneer.com/news/cm-rekha-launches-ev-subsidy-portal) — accessed 2026-10-04 — maturity: announced/launched.
- **Bottom line**: no source explicitly states "Switch Delhi was discontinued," but direct technical evidence (expired TLS cert, dead `/openev/` API) plus independent ground-check reporting (India Today) show clear neglect of the original locator/open-data infrastructure, even as raw charger counts nationally/city-wide keep growing via other channels (BSES, TPDDL, One Delhi app).
