## Novelty search log (claim 11)
| Query / check | Result |
|---|---|
| web search: "capture-recapture estimate completeness of electric vehicle charging station database" | Generic method explanation. Its one cited repo, `iboates/osm-charging-station-completeness` (1 star, last push 2023-10-01), measures OSM *tag* completeness for Karlsruhe, not a population estimate → not prior art |
| web search: "multiple systems estimation OpenStreetMap points of interest completeness capture recapture" | General MSE references (Bird & King 2018, public policy). OSM POI quality studies (Klinkhardt et al. 2023, large-scale real data; Crisafulli & Monzón 2021 OST thesis, machine learning). None applies MSE to charger databases |
| web search: "capture-recapture" OR "Lincoln-Petersen" "charging stations" infrastructure inventory | "no citation currently points to a peer-reviewed publication or technical report specifically estimating the undercount of electric vehicle charging stations using capture–recapture" (search engine summary; treated as weak evidence) |
| page check: eprints.ost.ch/id/eprint/940 | Completeness estimation of OSM POI data using machine learning; no capture/recapture terms on the page |
| research agent (`sota-methods.md` §g) | Reported an "explicit zero result" after several searches (not independently reproducible: its query log is incomplete) |

**Conclusion:** in 3 lead searches plus 2 close-candidate checks, we found no prior application of capture–recapture / multiple-systems estimation to EV charging-station inventories. The ingredients are classical (Lincoln–Petersen, Chapman, Fienberg log-linear models), so the novelty claim is the *application and its validation against official counts*, not the statistics.
